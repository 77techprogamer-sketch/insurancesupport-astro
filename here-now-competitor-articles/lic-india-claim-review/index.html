LIC of India Claim Settlement: What 8.48 Lakh Claims a Year Tell You About Rejections

Independent review for Indian policyholders · Facts from IRDAI public disclosures and insurer disclosures, FY 2023-24 & 2024-25

LIC of India settled 8,48,145 individual death claims in FY 2024-25, paying 97.08% of them within 30 days per IRDAI's published claim data — a scale no other Indian life insurer comes close to. But scale cuts both ways: when a fraction of that volume is rejected, it is still tens of thousands of families fighting for money they believed was guaranteed.

How LIC actually settles claims

LIC's death claim process runs through the branch that services the policy. The nominee intimates the claim with the death certificate, policy bond (or a non-loss declaration), and KYC/bank details. LIC's own disclosure shows claims older than three years from inception are settled fastest; claims filed within the three-year "early claim" window can be investigated, which is where most delays and rejections concentrate.

Why LIC claims get rejected

The recurring reasons are consistent across branches: non-disclosure of pre-existing illness or smoking at the proposal stage, a mismatch between the cause of death and policy terms, premium default leading to lapsed status, missing nominee updates after policy assignment, and incomplete paperwork in early claims. LIC's grievance data and IRDAI's lokpal referrals show a meaningful share of contested cases start with a lapsed-policy or material non-disclosure finding that the family disputes.

What most families miss: a revival request for a lapsed LIC policy is not automatic. LIC can demand fresh medical evidence and full back-premiums — and the death benefit for the pre-lapse period is only revived if the revival is accepted on the original terms.

Your appeal path on a rejected LIC claim

  • Stage 1 — Branch escalation: get the written rejection letter stating the exact clause relied upon. Oral rejections are not appealable; you need the document.
  • Stage 2 — LIC Grievance Redressal Cell: every zone office has one; the insurer must respond within the IRDAI-grievance timeline.
  • Stage 3 — IRDAI Bima Bharosa portal: if LIC does not resolve within the statutory period, file on IRDAI's portal. Life insurers must dispose of IRDAI-referred complaints within 14 days in most categories.
  • Stage 4 — Insurance Lokalayam / consumer forum: for amounts up to ₹50 lakh, the district consumer forum is fast and needs no lawyer.
