HDFC Life Claims: 99.50% Settled — And The 0.5% Where Non-Disclosure Decides Everything

Independent review for Indian policyholders · Facts from IRDAI public disclosures and insurer disclosures, FY 2022-25

HDFC Life publishes one of the strongest claim records among India's large private life insurers: 99.50% overall claim settlement ratio in FY 2024, disbursing ₹1,584 crore across 19,338 retail claims, and 99.97% of individual death claims settled within 30 days per IRDAI's Handbook — the best among large insurers by that measure. For a term-insurance buyer, that is a genuinely strong starting point. It is not, however, a promise about your claim.

How HDFC Life settles claims

Claimants can submit documents through multiple channels without a mandatory branch visit — app, portal, email or branch. HDFC Life offers same-day settlement for individual claims older than three years from policy inception when documents are complete and no investigation is required. Claims filed within three years can be investigated, and that is precisely where rejections and delays cluster.

Why HDFC Life claims get rejected

  • Non-disclosure in medical records: the single largest ground for private life-insurer rejections — tobacco, alcohol, existing conditions and family history found in hospital records at investigation.
  • Early claims (within 3 years): investigation timelines of 90-120 days are routine, and the outcome can be repudiation citing misrepresentation.
  • Premium default and grace-period lapses: a policy outside its grace period pays nothing but surrender value.
  • Beneficiary and assignment disputes: outdated nominees, assignments not updated, or contested family claims trigger delays.
Know the interest rule: IRDAI requires life insurers to pay interest at bank-rate-plus-2% per annum when a valid claim exceeds the settlement deadline. If HDFC Life's timeline breaches the statutory limit, the interest claim is part of your grievance — not an afterthought.

Your appeal path on a rejected HDFC Life claim

  • Get the repudiation letter naming the exact policy clause and the investigation findings relied upon.
  • Challenge the investigation report: hospital-record readings can be context-stripped, and "non-disclosure" claims are frequently rebutted with contemporaneous evidence (old policy records, employer health files, pharmacy history).
  • Escalate through HDFC Life's grievance cell, then IRDAI Bima Bharosa, then the Insurance Lokalayam or consumer forum.
  • For term claims above ₹50 lakh, the High Court is the realistic forum — but most reversals happen at the grievance stage.
