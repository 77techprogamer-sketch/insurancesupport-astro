Axis Max Life Claims: A 10-Day Guarantee, a 99.65% Ratio, and the Metric That Sits Below Both

Independent review for Indian policyholders · Facts from IRDAI public disclosures and insurer disclosures, FY 2022-25

Axis Max Life Insurance (formerly Max Life) settled 19,569 individual death claims worth ₹1,254 crore in FY 2023-24, its highest-ever claims paid ratio at 99.65% — up from 99.22% five years earlier. Its Claims Guarantee is the sharpest commitment in Indian life insurance: valid death claims are settled within 10 days, and if the insurer is late it pays interest at 10% per annum. It is worth understanding both what that guarantee covers and what it does not.

How Max Life settles claims

Intimation runs through phone, WhatsApp, portal or branch. The company says around 48% of non-early claims (older than three years) are settled same-day, with straight-through processing, OCR and API integrations under a claims transformation program aimed at 3-hour processing for 30% of death claims. IRDAI's separate within-30-days metric tells a different story at scale: 94.41% in FY 2024-25, below the 97.10% industry average — the fastest insurer on its own guarantee, mid-pack on the regulator's clock.

Why Max Life claims get rejected

  • Non-disclosure found by fraud-detection systems: Max Life states it uses analytics to flag high-risk geographies and false-claim probability from non-disclosure — early claims are where these flags bite.
  • Incomplete documentation at intimation: the 10-day guarantee applies only to complete, valid claims; a missing death certificate original or bank proof resets the clock.
  • Premium lapse outside the grace period: surrender value only, no death benefit.
  • Cause-of-death and policy-condition mismatches: accident-benefit riders and term plans differ sharply on what death circumstances they cover.
Use the guarantee as leverage: if your valid claim with complete documents passes day 10, the 10% per annum interest entitlement is written into the company's own commitment. Cite it in the first grievance — it changes how quickly a claims file gets reopened.

Your appeal path on a rejected Max Life claim

  • Get the written repudiation naming the clause and the documents or findings relied upon.
  • Rebut non-disclosure findings with dated evidence predating the proposal — old medical files, employer records, pharmacy history.
  • Escalate through Max Life's grievance cell, then IRDAI Bima Bharosa portal within the statutory period.
  • Insurance Lokalayam or district consumer forum for amounts up to ₹50 lakh.
