ICICI Lombard Claims: The 86.86% Settlement Ratio and What It Means When Yours Is the 13%

Independent review for Indian policyholders · Facts from IRDAI public disclosures and insurer disclosures, FY 2023-24 & 2024-25

ICICI Lombard handles one of India's largest general-insurance claim volumes — over 28.9 lakh claims were intimated with it in FY 2023-24 alone. Its in-house claim settlement (86.7% of health claims, per its annual disclosure) and the IL Take Care app made it a default choice for urban buyers. The same disclosure data shows why claim quality, not claim speed, is the battleground: ICICI Lombard's health claim settlement ratio by number was 86.86% in FY 2023-24, below the ~93% industry average.

How ICICI Lombard settles claims

Cashless pre-authorization runs through the IL Take Care app or the hospital's insurance desk; reimbursement claims go through the same app with scanned bills. The insurer uses in-house health claims handling for most of its book and published a Claims NPS of 67 with a first-call resolution rate of 83.74% — strong operational numbers that coexist with a below-average settlement ratio by count.

The gap between CSR by number (86.86%) and by amount (68.49%) is the number to watch: it means smaller claims clear far more often than large ones — exactly the claims where hospitals press families hardest.

Why ICICI Lombard claims get rejected

  • Pre-existing disease non-disclosure: underwriting telemedicine or online-purchased policies skip medicals, and the disclosure gap surfaces at claim time.
  • Sub-limits and room-rent caps: a private-room upgrade pushes ICU and surgeon charges above the cap, triggering proportionality deductions.
  • Non-network hospital cashless denial: cashless is only guaranteed at empanelled hospitals; everywhere else it is reimbursement.
  • 30-day initial waiting and 2-4 year disease-specific waits: cataract and maternity waits catch buyers who never read the schedule.
Proportionality is reversible: most ICICI Lombard "partial rejections" on room-rent grounds are deduction notices, not final denials. IRDAI's grievance route and the Insurance Act's by-default-payment rule (section 64VB read with sector rules) are your leverage — and IRDAI's 2026 pre-authorization rules tightened timelines that insurers must now meet.

Your appeal path on a rejected ICICI Lombard claim

  • Request the claim file: under IRDAI's grievance rules you are entitled to the assessment letter and reasons in writing.
  • Escalate through ICICI Lombard's grievance cell, then the IRDAI Bima Bharosa portal if unresolved.
  • For proportionality deductions, contest with the bill-vs-slab computation — most reversals happen here, before any forum.
  • Consumer forum for final denials above ₹10 lakh of claimed amount; the district forum handles up to ₹50 lakh.
