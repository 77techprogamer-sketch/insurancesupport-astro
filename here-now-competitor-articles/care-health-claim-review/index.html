Care Health Claims Reviewed: A 93% Settlement Ratio With One Number Worth Watching

Independent review for Indian policyholders · Facts from IRDAI public disclosures and insurer disclosures, FY 2022-25

Care Health Insurance (formerly Religare Health) is a standalone health insurer that has held a claim settlement ratio around 93% across FY 2022-25 — at or above the industry average — on a book of roughly ₹8,064 crore annual premium income in FY 2024-25. With 11,000+ network hospitals and products like Care Supreme and Care Freedom in its portfolio, it is a serious alternative to the composite insurers for family floater buyers.

How Care Health settles claims

Cashless claims run through its network hospital desk and the Care Health app; reimbursement claims are filed with scanned bills. Two numbers define its profile: a CSR of ~93% (92.81% by number in FY 2023-24) alongside an incurred claim ratio of ~59% — far below the 82% industry average. A low ICR with a high CSR usually signals disciplined claim assessment, and Care's complaint rate of 42 per 10,000 claims sits above the industry's 27.

Why Care Health claims get rejected

  • Pre-existing condition disclosure: tele-underwritten policies surface PCN/PED findings at claim time.
  • Disease-specific waiting periods: Care plans carry 2-4 year waits for specified illnesses; cataract and maternity waits are shorter but real.
  • Room category upgrades: proportionality deductions on surgeon/ICU charges follow a single-private-room cap.
  • Plan-specific sub-limits: Care Freedom and some senior-citizen variants cap sum insured and carry sub-limits that reduce payouts.
The ICR angle: a 59% incurred claim ratio against an 82% industry average means Care pays out far less of its premium than peers. That is what makes its below-industry complaint rate (42 vs 27 per 10,000) worth understanding before you buy — and worth documenting carefully before you claim.

Your appeal path on a rejected Care Health claim

  • Get the written assessment citing the exact clause — the first document every Care claimant should insist on.
  • Escalate through Care's grievance cell with the clause-by-clause rebuttal.
  • File on IRDAI Bima Bharosa if unresolved within the statutory period — standalone health insurers answer to the same timelines.
  • District consumer forum for final denials up to ₹50 lakh; no lawyer needed at that tier.
