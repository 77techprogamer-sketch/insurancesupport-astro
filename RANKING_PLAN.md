## PART 1: WHY YOU'RE NOT RANKING (The Real Issues)

### 1. Domain Authority Is Zero
This is the #1 reason. You're a brand-new domain with:
- No meaningful backlinks
- No mentions in insurance/finance media
- No Wikipedia or authoritative reference presence
- Google has no reason to trust you yet

**Hard truth:** No amount of on-page optimization will get a zero-authority domain to rank for competitive terms. You need links AND time.

### 2. The Site Is Still Recovering from Algorithmic Suppression
- Old spam pages (536 machine-translated + link scheme) triggered Google's filters
- You've cleaned up (noindex, disavow, removed satellites) — good
- But Google needs to re-crawl and re-assess — this takes weeks/months
- Sitemap has 197 URLs but only ~200 were previously indexed (mixed with spam)

### 3. Content Is Good But Not Differentiated Enough
Your content covers standard insurance topics well, but:
- Many articles follow similar structures to competitors
- Stats without clear primary sources (even after our fixes)
- Limited original data/research that would earn links naturally
- "Hari Kotian" brand not yet recognized as an authority by AI systems

### 4. You're Competing in Highly Competitive Space
| Keyword Cluster | Competition | Your Realistic Position |
|-----------------|-------------|-------------------------|
| "insurance claim rejection" | Very High (Policybazaar, Acko, major insurers) | Long-tail only |
| "LIC policy revival" | High (LIC itself, Policybazaar, BankBazaar) | Medium-tail possible |
| "IRDAI grievance process" | Medium (IRDAI site, insurers, legal sites) | **Your best opportunity** |

GBP rankings drive local pack results AND influence organic. For "insurance advisor near me" or "insurance help Bengaluru," this is your #1 channel.

#### E. Build Topical Authority Through Content Clusters

**Current state:** You have 89 blog posts spread across many topics. This is "scattergun" — good for breadth, bad for depth.

**Better approach:** Build topic clusters around your best opportunities:

**Cluster 1: IRDAI & Regulatory (YOUR NICHE)**
- Pillar: "Complete IRDAI Guide for Policyholders 2026"
- Subtopics: grievance process, IGMS portal, Ombudsman, cashless rules, claim settlement regs, standardized policy wordings, solvency ratio, how to check insurer credentials
- Internal links: all subtopics → pillar, pillar → all subtopics
- Target: Become THE site for IRDAI regulatory info

**Cluster 2: Claim Recovery (YOUR CORE)**
- Pillar: "Insurance Claim Recovery: Complete Guide"
- Subtopics: health claim rejection, LIC claim rejection, motor claim denied, travel claim denied, cashless denial, pre-auth denied, appeal process, documentation, Ombudsman, IGMS
- Internal links: cluster tightly
- Target: Rank for all claim rejection long-tails

**Cluster 3: LIC Policies (YOUR DIFFERENTIATION)**
- Pillar: "Complete LIC Guide 2026"
- Subtopics: policy revival, maturity claims, death claims, Jeevan Anand vs Labh, latest plans, premium payment modes, loans against LIC, lapsed policy after 5 years
- Target: LIC content has less competition than generic insurance

#### F. Add Schema That Actually Helps

You have good schema already (Organization, Person, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList). Add:

| Schema Type | Where | Why It Helps |
|-------------|-------|--------------|
| **HowTo** on claim process guides | Blog posts with step-by-step processes | Rich results + AI can extract steps |
| **Article** with full metadata on ALL posts | Blog posts | Author, date, revision history — E-E-A-T |
| **Service** schema on service pages | `/services/*` | Describes your services to search engines |
| **FAQPage** on every FAQ-rich page | Service pages, guides | Voice search + AI extraction |
| **Person** with IRDAI credential on author bylines | Every blog post | E-E-A-T for YMYL content |

## PART 4: WHAT NOT TO DO

❌ **Don't chase high-volume keywords** — "best health insurance India" has 0 chance. Target specific problems.
❌ **Don't buy links or use link networks** — you JUST recovered from algorithmic suppression. Don't do it again.
❌ **Don't create more thin content** — 89 quality posts > 500 thin ones.
❌ **Don't expect quick wins** — zero-authority domain = slow start. Play the long game.
❌ **Don't ignore GBP** — it's your fastest path to actual traffic.
❌ **Don't rely on AI overviews alone** — build for traditional search too. AI visibility is bonus, not strategy.
