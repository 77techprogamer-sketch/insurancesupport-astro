

<h2>IRDAI Claim Settlement Regulations and Your Rights</h2>

<p>The <strong>IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) Regulations, 2017</strong> (as amended through 2026) guarantee every policyholder enforceable rights when filing a claim. These regulations mandate that insurers must settle claims within <strong>30 days of receiving all required documents</strong>. Any delay beyond 30 days exposes the insurer to a compulsory penalty interest of 2% above the prevailing bank rate.</p>

<p>Under Section 45 of the Insurance Act, 1938, no life insurance claim can be called into question after <strong>3 years of continuous coverage</strong> from the date of policy issuance, except in cases of proven fraud. For health insurance claims, the IRDAI <strong>moratorium period</strong> of eight years protects policyholders: after eight continuous years, insurers cannot repudiate claims on grounds of non-disclosure or misrepresentation, except fraud.</p>

<p>These statutory protections are enforced through the <strong>IRDAI Bima Bharosa portal</strong> (bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in, formerly IGMS). If an insurer fails to resolve a claim dispute within the stipulated period, policyholders can log a complaint online and expect acknowledgment with a reference number. The Insurance Ombudsman handles disputes up to ₹30 lakh without court fees, and its decisions are binding on the insurer.</p>

<h2>Practical Example / Case Study: Resolving a Delayed LIC Maturity Claim in Bangalore</h2>

<p><strong>Case Background:</strong> Mr. Anil Kumar, a 62-year-old retired government employee in Jayanagar, Bangalore, submitted a maturity claim for LIC Jeevan Anand (Policy No. XXXXX, Sum Assured ₹15 Lakh) 45 days before the maturity date. Despite submitting all required documents (original policy bond, NEFT mandate form, PAN, and Aadhaar copies), the portal continued to show "Claim Intimated" with no movement for over 25 days.</p>

<p><strong>Investigation & Intervention:</strong> Our Bangalore advisory team checked the claim details and identified that the NEFT mandate form was filled without the IFSC code and had a mismatched account holder name (initials versus full name). We guided Mr. Kumar to resubmit the corrected documents, filed a preliminary grievance through the Bima Bharosa portal citing IRDAI's mandatory 30-day settlement rule, and also wrote directly to LIC's Chief Claims Officer.</p>

<p><strong>Outcome:</strong> Within 10 days of resubmission, the portal status updated to "Approved" and subsequently "Settled." The maturity amount of ₹22.8 lakh (Sum Assured ₹15L + Reversionary Bonuses ₹7.1L + FAB ₹70,000) was credited directly to Mr. Kumar's bank account via NEFT, within the IRDAI-mandated timeline.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<p><strong>Q1: Can I track my LIC death claim status online?</strong><br>A: Yes. Death claim status can be tracked via the LIC e-Services portal, SMS (send "LICHELP [policy no] CLAIM" to 9222492224), or by calling LIC helpline 022-68276827.</p>

<p><strong>Q2: Why is my LIC claim status showing "Pending Investigation"?</strong><br>A: Early claims (within 3 years of policy issuance) undergo mandatory investigation per IRDAI norms. This can take up to 90 days.</p>

<p><strong>Q3: What if LIC rejects my claim—what are my options?</strong><br>A: You can appeal through LIC's internal grievance cell, file a complaint on IRDAI Bima Bharosa portal, or approach the Insurance Ombudsman (for claims up to ₹30 lakh) or Consumer Forum.</p>

<p><strong>Q4: Can I check claim status without registering on the LIC portal?</strong><br>A: Yes, use the quick-access "Claim Status" tool on licindia.in by entering your policy number, date of birth, and premium amount.</p>

<p><strong>Q5: Is the LIC SMS service free?</strong><br>A: Standard SMS charges from your telecom operator apply. The LIC response is automated and free of charge.</p>

<p><strong>Q6: How long does LIC take to settle a maturity claim?</strong><br>A: Standard maturity claims are typically settled within 8-15 working days from the date of receipt of all complete documents. Any delay beyond 30 days incurs penalty interest per IRDAI regulations.</p>
