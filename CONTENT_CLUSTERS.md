## CLUSTER INTERNAL LINKING RULES

### Every Article Should:
1. Link to its pillar page (first 2 paragraphs + CTA at end)
2. Link to 2-4 related articles within the SAME cluster
3. Link to 1-2 articles in OTHER clusters where relevant (cross-cluster links build site-wide authority)
4. Be linked FROM the pillar page (pillar links to all cluster members)

### Pillar Pages Should:
1. Link to every article in the cluster (organized by subtopic)
2. Link to related clusters (e.g., IRDAI cluster → Claim Recovery cluster)
3. Include a "Related guides" section at the bottom
4. Be linked from homepage or main blog page

### Homepage Should:
1. Link to all 3 pillar pages (or at least the most important ones)
2. Link to top 5-10 blog posts
3. This distributes authority from homepage to cluster pillars
