Total Loss Motor Claim: 9.5 Lakh Settled After IDV Dispute

Bajaj Allianz offered 60% of IDV for a flood-damaged car. We negotiated full IDV citing IRDAI guidelines.

Summary

This account and its amounts are reported by Insurance Support and have not been independently verified. Details are selective, and the outcome of another claim may differ.

After Bangalore's July 2024 floods, a Koramangala resident's car was completely submerged. The surveyor declared it a total loss (repair cost > 75% IDV). Bajaj Allianz offered only 60% of IDV, claiming 'partial damage'. We contested with the surveyor's own report, IRDAI Motor Tariff guidelines, and got full IDV within 35 days.

How We Solved It

S Situation

Car submerged in 5 feet of water for 48 hours during Bangalore floods. Independent surveyor confirmed total loss — repair estimate 8.2 lakh vs IDV 9.5 lakh. Insurer's in-house assessor downgraded to 'partial damage' to reduce payout.

T Task

Prove the vehicle meets total loss criteria (>75% of IDV) per IRDAI Motor Vehicles Tariff. Get full IDV instead of 60% offer.

A Action

  1. 1.Obtained independent surveyor's total loss certificate with photos
  2. 2.Cited IRDAI Motor Tariff: 'constructive total loss if repair > 75% IDV'
  3. 3.Submitted flood water level reports from BBMP for the area
  4. 4.Got the insurer's own surveyor to confirm total loss in writing
  5. 5.Escalated to GRO with IRDAI circular reference on flood claims

R Result

Bajaj Allianz GRO approved full IDV of 9,50,000 within 35 days. The approval cited the independent surveyor's report as decisive.

Lessons Learned

Lessons Learned

  • Total loss = repair cost > 75% IDV (IRDAI rule, not insurer's opinion)
  • Independent surveyor trumps in-house assessor
  • Government flood reports strengthen the case
  • Escalate to GRO with specific IRDAI circular references
