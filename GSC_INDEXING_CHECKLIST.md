# Google "Request Indexing" — Priority URL Checklist

**How to use (do this weekly, Google allows re-requesting periodically):**

1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) → select your property (`insurancesupport.online`).
2. Paste **one URL at a time** into the **URL Inspection** bar at the top.
3. Click **"Request Indexing"** (the blue button that appears after the check).
4. Work through the priority list each week. Google only lets you request a handful per week — prioritize the TOP 10 first, then rotate the rest in over subsequent weeks.
5. Also go to **Sitemaps** in the sidebar and re-submit `https://insurancesupport.online/sitemap.xml`.

Google gives limited "Request Indexing" requests — use them on these pages, **not** on the 200-URL sitemap.

## Frequency & pacing

- **Week 1:** Tier 1 (10 URLs) + 10 of Tier 2 = 20 requests.
- **Week 2:** Remaining Tier 2 + first 10 of Tier 3.
- **Week 3+:** Rotate through the rest. Re-request the homepage + top claims posts every 2 weeks.
- The sitemap handles the other ~180 URLs automatically over time — you don't need to request those manually.

## What to watch

- In GSC → **Pages** report, watch the "Discovered – currently not indexed" and "Crawled – currently not indexed" tabs. Those are pages Google knows about but hasn't indexed yet; a successful "Request Indexing" moves them toward being indexed.
- If a page shows "Request Indexing" is unavailable (cooldown), wait a few days.
- **Indexed pages only stick if they get crawled repeatedly** — the real driver is your Google Business Profile, reviews, and backlinks. Requesting indexing without those is like watering a plant with no soil.
