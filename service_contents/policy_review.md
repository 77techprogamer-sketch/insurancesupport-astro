## Policy Review: Complete 2026 Guide for Insurance Policy Audit and Optimization

### Why Your Insurance Policy Needs Annual Review

Most Indians buy insurance and forget about it for years. According to IRDAI 2026 data, 45% of insurance policies in India are either outdated, inadequate, or no longer aligned with the policyholder's needs. An annual policy review can save you 15-25% on premiums while ensuring adequate coverage.

**1. Coverage Adequacy**

### Common Policy Issues We Find

1. **Outdated Sum Insured**: Many policies have sum insured not adjusted for inflation over 5-10 years
2. **Missing Critical Illness Cover**: Less than 20% of health policies include critical illness cover
3. **Inadequate Motor Coverage**: Vehicles insured at market value instead of IDV
4. **No Personal Accident Cover**: Essential add-on often overlooked
5. **Wrong Nominee Details**: Outdated nominee information leading to claim delays
