## Child Education Plan: Complete 2026 Guide for Securing Your Child's Future

### Why Education Planning Cannot Wait

Education inflation in India is increasing at 10-12% annually. A professional degree today costs ₹20-50 lakhs, and by 2035 it will cost ₹80 lakhs to ₹2 crores. According to IRDAI 2026 data, only 22% of Indian parents have started systematic education planning for their children before age 5.

### Types of Child Education Plans

**1. Child Insurance Plans (Endowment)**
- Guaranteed returns along with life cover
- Premiums paid during child's growing years
- Maturity benefits available when child reaches 18-25 years
- Tax benefits under Section 80C and 10(10D)

**2. Child ULIP Plans**
- Market-linked returns with insurance coverage
- Potentially higher returns than endowment (8-12% average)
- Risk of market volatility
- Better for long-term planning (10+ years)

**3. Education Savings Plans**
- Pure investment without insurance component
- Higher returns than insurance-linked plans
- Flexibility to withdraw partially at milestones
- Recommended for financially savvy parents

**4. Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (For Girl Child)**
- Government-backed scheme with 8.2% guaranteed returns
- Tax benefits under Section 80C
- Available for girls below 10 years
- Lock-in until age 21

### Claim Process for Child Education Plans

1. **Maturity Claim**: Submit policy document, child's age proof, and claim form at maturity date
2. **Partial Withdrawal**: Available after 5 years for specific educational expenses
3. **Death Benefit**: If parent dies during policy term, future premiums waived and child receives maturity at designated age
4. **Documentation**: Age proof of child, policy document, bank details for direct credit

### Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the best age to start child education planning?** The earlier, the better. Starting at child's birth gives maximum time for compounding. A ₹5,000 monthly investment from birth can accumulate ₹25-30 lakhs by age 18.

**Should I choose endowment or ULIP for child education?** For conservative parents, endowment plans provide guaranteed returns. For parents comfortable with market risk, ULIPs offer potentially higher returns. Many experts recommend a combination of both.

**Can I use PPF for child education?** Yes, PPF offers 7.5% tax-free returns with 15-year lock-in. It is one of the safest options for long-term child education planning.

**What if I cannot continue premium payments?** Most child plans offer premium waiver benefit where future premiums are waived if the parent dies during the term, and the policy continues until maturity.

**How do I track if I am on track?** We provide annual progress reports comparing your accumulated corpus with the target amount, with specific recommendations if you are behind.

**Should I buy term insurance along with child plan?** Yes. A separate term plan ensures your child's education is protected even if something happens to you, while the child plan provides dedicated education corpus.
