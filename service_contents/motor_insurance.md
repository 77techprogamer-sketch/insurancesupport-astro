## Motor Insurance Complete Guide for Cars and Bikes in India (2026)

### Motor Insurance Legal Requirements in India

The Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, amended in 2019, mandates third-party insurance for all vehicles plying on Indian roads. This requirement extends to private cars, commercial vehicles, and two-wheelers. IRDAI regulations for 2026 introduced stricter penalties for uninsured vehicles including immediate vehicle impoundment, fines up to ₹10,000, and imprisonment up to 3 months.

### Types of Motor Insurance Coverage

1. **Third-Party Liability Insurance**: The bare minimum required by law. Covers damage caused to third parties (property damage, bodily injury, death claims). Does not cover your own vehicle.

2. **Comprehensive Insurance**: The most complete coverage, combining third-party liability with own damage protection. Covers theft, fire, natural disasters, vandalism, and accident damage to your vehicle.

3. **Collision Damage Waiver (CDW)**: Add-on covering repair costs without paying the deductible when your vehicle is damaged in a collision.

4. **Zero Depreciation Cover**: Add-on that removes depreciation deductions typically applied to parts like batteries, AC components, nylon parts, and plastic components.

5. **Engine Protection Cover**: Covers engine damage caused by water ingression, lubrication failure, or mechanical breakdown—typically excluded from standard warranties.

6. **Personal Accident Cover**: Mandatory for owner-driver (₹5 lakhs standard), covers driver and sometimes passengers in case of injury or death.

### Claim Process Timeline (IRDAi Rules 2026)

1. **Immediate notification**: Within 24 hours of incident using the insurer's 24x7 claims helpline
2. **Digital claim intimation**: Through insurer's mobile app or website within 24 hours
3. **Surveyor inspection**: Within 24-48 hours for major accidents (online assessment available for minor damages)
4. **Document submission**: All supporting documents within 7 working days
5. **Claim decision**: Within 30 days for cashless claims, 45 days for reimbursement claims
6. **Disbursement**: Within 7 working days of approval

If the insurer fails to meet timelines without valid reason, IRDAi mandates 12% interest on delayed claims plus a penalty.

### Claim Documents Required

- Original RC (Registration Certificate) and driving license
- FIR for theft or third-party accidents (within 24 hours)
- Original policy document and RC copy
- Duly filled claim form
- Two-witness statements (for theft cases)
- Original parking receipt (for theft claims)
- Repairing charges bills (in case of reimbursement)
- Intimation number (obtained immediately after calling insurer)

### Zero Depreciation Cover: Detailed Breakdown

Standard motor insurance policies deduct depreciation from claim amounts for certain parts:

| Part | Depreciation Rate | Age Limit |
|------|-------------------|-----------|
| Fiberglass components | 30% | Up to 5 years |
| Aluminium components | 50% | Up to 5 years |
| Nylon/plastic parts | 50% | Up to 5 years |
| Acrylic/glass parts | 10-25% | Up to 5 years |
| Labor costs | 5% per year | Up to 3 years |
| Battery | 20% | Up to 5 years |
| Air conditioning gas | 5-10% | Up to 5 years |

Zero depreciation cover costs 20-30% extra but eliminates all these deductions, making it worthwhile for vehicles older than 2 years.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I claim if my bike is stolen from Koramangala?**

Yes, theft claims are covered. File FIR within 24 hours, then claim intimation within 24 hours. We help coordinate with local police stations in Bangalore.

**What is IDV and why does it matter?**

Insured Declared Value (IDV) is the maximum amount insurers pay for total loss or theft. Higher IDV = higher premium but also higher claim amount. Review IDV annually during policy renewal.

**Can I claim for accidental damage to my own car?**

Yes, with comprehensive coverage including Zero Depreciation. You pay actual cash value (ACV) which includes depreciation, making Zero Dep cover essential.

**What is the difference between cashless and reimbursement claims?**

Cashless claims settle directly between insurer and network garage. Reimbursement requires you to pay upfront, then claim reimbursement.

**Do I need insurance for a new car before registration?**

Yes, temporary insurance valid for 30 days from date of purchase is required during registration process.

**Can I claim for accessories like helmet or GPS?**

Only if declared at policy purchase. Standard policies exclude non-declared accessories. Add Personal Belongings Cover (₹5,000-25,000) for coverage.
