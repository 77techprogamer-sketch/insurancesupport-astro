## Term Insurance: 2026 Complete Guide for Indian Families

### Why Term Insurance Is the Smartest Financial Decision

Term insurance provides the highest life cover at the lowest cost. A 30-year-old earning ₹12 lakhs/year in Bangalore can secure a ₹1 crore term plan for just ₹25,000-45,000 per year—less than 0.4% of income. Compare to endowment plans where the same coverage costs ₹80,000-1,20,000 annually.

The fundamental principle behind term insurance is simple: pay a small premium for pure protection, and invest the savings difference elsewhere for higher returns. This approach, advocated by Nobel laureate economists, outperforms endowment-linked insurance by 40-60% in total returns over 25 years.

### Understanding Term Insurance Structure

Term insurance provides a death benefit (sum assured) payable to nominees if the insured dies during the policy term. Unlike whole life or endowment plans, there is no maturity benefit—you pay only for the risk cover. This is why premiums are 10-15x cheaper than equivalent coverage from savings-linked products.

**Key Features of Term Insurance (2026 IRDAI Regulations)**:

- Sum assured range: ₹25 lakhs to ₹25 crores
- Policy terms: 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, or up to 99 years
- Premium payment: Single pay, regular pay, limited pay, or pay till 60/65
- Riders: Critical illness, accidental death, waiver of premium, income benefit
- Tax benefits: Section 80C (premiums) and Section 10(10D) (death benefit)

### Inbuilt Benefits (2026 IRDAI Rules)

1. **Waiver of Premium**: Automatically pays future premiums if policyholder dies or becomes disabled (waiver amount up to ₹50 lakhs)

2. **Accidental Death Benefit**: Double payout if death occurs due to accident (add ₹1,500-2,500/year to premium)

3. **Critical Illness Rider**: Lump sum payout on diagnosis of listed critical illnesses (add ₹3,000-8,000/year)

4. **Partial Withdrawal**: Available for specific life events like home purchase, education expenses (available after 5 years, up to 25% of sum assured)

5. **Income Replacement**: Monthly income benefit for family (₹10,000-50,000/month for 10 years after death)

### Health Disclosure Requirements

During application, IRDAI requires disclosure of:

- Previous medical history (surgical procedures, chronic conditions)
- Family medical history (hereditary diseases)
- Lifestyle habits (smoking, drinking, hazardous activities)
- BMI and blood pressure readings
- Smoking status (affects premium by 50-100%)

Non-disclosure can lead to claim rejection. Always be transparent—insurers verify medical records during claim settlement.

### Claim Process (Step by Step)

1. **Intimate the company**: Within 30 days of death (immediate notification helps)
2. **Submit claim form**: Along with death certificate, discharge summary, and policy documents
3. **Document verification**: Insurer checks policy terms and documentation completeness
4. **Investigation**: Medical records verification (if death is unnatural or within first 3 years)
5. **Decision**: Within 30 days of complete document submission
6. **Disbursement**: Within 7 working days of approval (delay attracts 12% interest penalty)

### Common Reasons for Claim Rejection (and Prevention)

1. **Non-disclosure of smoking status**: Disclose if you smoke—even social smoking. Premium adjusts by 50-100%, but claim is secure.

2. **Undiagnosed pre-existing conditions**: Always mention known diagnoses even if "doctor said nothing serious."

3. **Lapse in premium payment**: Ensure premium auto-debit is set up. Missing 30+ days lapses policy.

4. **Suicide within first year**: Claims rejected if death occurs within 12 months of policy issuance.

5. **Driving under influence**: If death occurs while driving under influence of alcohol or drugs, claim rejected.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**What age can I buy term insurance?**

Minimum 18 years (with guardian consent for minors). Maximum entry age varies by insurer (up to 55-60 for most). Most popular entry ages are 25-40 years.

**Can I take a loan against my term insurance?**

No, term plans don't have a surrender value component. This is a key difference from endowment or ULIP plans.

**What happens if I miss a premium payment?**

Grace period of 15-30 days applies (varies by insurer). During this period, coverage continues. After grace period, policy lapses and coverage ends completely. Premium financing options available for high-value policies.

**Can I change my nominee?**

Yes, anytime during the policy term by submitting a nomination change form along with age proof of new nominee. Changes take effect immediately upon acknowledgment.

**Is term insurance really necessary if I have employer-provided insurance?**

Yes. Employer-provided group insurance is temporary and tied to employment. Term insurance provides lifelong protection regardless of job changes, especially important for families relying on your income.

**How does smoking affect my premium?**

Smokers pay 50-100% higher premiums than non-smokers. If you quit smoking for 12+ consecutive months, you can request a premium reduction with documentation.

**What is the difference between level term and decreasing term?**

Level term provides fixed sum assured throughout the policy. Decreasing term reduces sum assured annually (usually tied to outstanding loan balance). Level term is recommended for most families.

**Can I buy term insurance online?**

Yes, IRDAI allows 100% online purchase of term insurance. Online plans often offer 10-15% lower premiums than offline plans due to lower distribution costs.
