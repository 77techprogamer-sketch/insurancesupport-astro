### Group Health Insurance (Mandatory Benefits Under 2026 Rules)

The Employee State Insurance (ESI) Act 1988, amended in 2026, requires businesses with 10+ employees to provide health insurance coverage. For businesses below this threshold, IRDAI recommends voluntary group health insurance.

**Key Benefits of Group Health Insurance for SMEs**:

- **Employee retention**: 72% employees consider health insurance a key benefit
- **Tax benefits**: Premiums deductible under Section 37(1) of Income Tax Act
- **Family coverage**: Extended to spouse, children (up to 2), and dependent parents
- **Cashless hospitalization**: Access to 10,000+ network hospitals across India
- **No waiting period**: Pre-existing conditions covered from Day 1

### Professional Indemnity Insurance

Covers legal liability arising from professional errors, omissions, or negligent advice. Essential for:

- **IT/Software companies**: Coverage for system failures, data loss, intellectual property disputes
- **Consultants/Advisors**: Protection against claims of faulty advice or bad recommendations
- **CA/Legal firms**: Coverage for errors in financial statements or legal opinions
- **Architects/Engineers**: Protection against design defects and project delays

**Claim Examples**:

- IT company faced ₹25 lakh claim for server downtime during client's product launch
- Management consultant paid ₹15 lakhs for advice that led to client's financial loss
- Marketing agency settled ₹8 lakhs for incorrect campaign claims

### Cyber Insurance for Small Businesses

Cyber attacks on Indian SMEs increased 300% between 2022-2025. Average cost per incident: ₹2-5 crores. Cyber insurance covers:

- **Data breach response**: Forensic investigation, legal counsel, customer notification costs
- **Business interruption**: Lost revenue during system downtime
- **Ransomware**: Payment of ransom demands and negotiation assistance
- **Regulatory penalties**: Fines under Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023
- **Third-party liability**: Claims from customers whose data was compromised

**Premium Factors**: Number of employees, revenue, industry, data sensitivity, cybersecurity measures in place.

### Business Interruption Insurance

Covers lost revenue when business operations are disrupted due to:

- Fire, flood, earthquake, or natural disasters
- Equipment breakdown
- Supply chain disruption
- Government-mandated closures

**Coverage Period**: Typically 6-12 months of average monthly revenue. Premium: 1-2% of annual revenue.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**What insurance does an SME with 10 employees need?**

At minimum: Group health insurance (mandatory in many states), professional indemnity (for service businesses), and general liability (for client-facing operations). We recommend starting with Group Health + Professional Indemnity for ₹50,000-80,000 total annual premium.

**Can I get a single package covering all SME risks?**

Yes, IRDAI-approved Micro, Small & Medium Enterprise (MSME) packages combine multiple coverages into one affordable premium. Package policies offer 15-25% savings compared to individual policies.

**Is cyber insurance necessary for small businesses?**

Yes. 43% of cyber attacks target small businesses. A single data breach can cost ₹15-30 lakhs in recovery, legal fees, and customer notification. Cyber insurance premiums start at ₹25,000/year for ₹1 crore coverage.

**How do I calculate the right coverage amount for my SME?**

Start with: Annual revenue × 2 (for business interruption) + Potential liability exposure (typically 1-3% of revenue) + Employee health coverage (₹2-10 lakhs per employee). We provide free risk assessment for Bangalore SMEs.

**What is the claim process for SME insurance?**

Most insurers offer online claim submission. For health claims: use network hospitals for cashless treatment. For liability claims: notify insurer within 48 hours, provide all supporting documents. We handle entire claim process for SME clients.

**Can I add employees to group health insurance mid-year?**

Yes, most group health policies allow adding new employees at pro-rata premium. However, some insurers may have minimum notice periods (30-60 days). We help coordinate mid-year additions efficiently.

**What are tax benefits of SME insurance?**

Group health premiums: Fully deductible under Section 37(1). Professional indemnity: Fully deductible under Section 37(1). Group personal accident: Fully deductible. Cyber insurance: Fully deductible as business expense.
