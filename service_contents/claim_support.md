### Step-by-Step Claim Process

**Step 1: Claim Notification (Immediate)**
- Call insurer's 24x7 claims helpline within 24-48 hours
- For motor claims: File FIR if theft or third-party involvement
- For health claims: Inform hospital for cashless arrangement

**Step 2: Documentation**
- Collect all required documents based on claim type
- We provide a checklist specific to your claim
- Ensure documents are original or certified copies

**Step 3: Submission**
- Submit at nearest branch or online portal
- We track submission and provide reference number
- Regular follow-up until settlement

**Step 4: Surveyor/Investigation**
- For motor claims: Surveyor inspection within 48 hours
- For health claims: Medical records verification
- For life claims: Hospital records cross-check

**Step 5: Settlement Decision**
- Within 30 days for cashless health claims
- Within 45 days for reimbursement claims
- Within 30 days for life insurance claims

**Step 6: Disbursement**
- Within 7 working days of approval
- Direct bank transfer or DD as per preference

### IRDAI Grievance Process (If Claim Rejected)

If your claim is rejected, we follow a 3-tier resolution process:

1. **Internal Appeal**: File with insurer's grievance cell (response within 15 days)
2. **Insurance Ombudsman**: File complaint with regional ombudsman if unsatisfied
3. **IRDAI IGMS**: Use IRDAI Integrated Grievance Management System for regulatory intervention
