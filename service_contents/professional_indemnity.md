## Professional Indemnity: Complete 2026 Guide for Service Businesses

### Why Professional Indemnity Insurance Is Essential

Professional indemnity insurance protects service-based businesses against claims of negligence, errors, or omissions in their professional advice or services. According to IRDAI 2026 data, 78% of professional liability claims in India involve documentation errors, 15% involve financial losses due to bad advice, and 7% involve breach of duty of care.

### Who Needs Professional Indemnity Insurance

**Essential Coverage For:**
- IT/Software companies (system failures, data loss, IP disputes)
- Consultants and advisors (faulty advice, incorrect recommendations)
- Chartered Accountants (financial statement errors, tax computation mistakes)
- Legal firms (incorrect legal advice, missed deadlines)
- Architects and engineers (design defects, project delays)
- Medical professionals (medical negligence, wrong diagnosis)
- Financial advisors (incorrect investment advice, portfolio losses)

### Types of Professional Indemnity Coverage

**1. Claims-Made Policy**
- Covers claims made during the policy period
- Most common and affordable option
- Retroactive date covers previous work
- Requires continuous renewal for gap-free coverage

**2. Occurrence-Based Policy**
- Covers incidents that occurred during policy period
- More expensive but broader protection
- No need for continuous renewal
- Suitable for high-risk professions

**3. Extended Reporting Period**
- Covers claims made after policy expiry
- For incidents that occurred during active policy
- Typically available for 60-90 days after cancellation
- Important for retiring professionals

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Is professional indemnity mandatory?** Not mandatory for all professions, but required for certain regulated professions (CA, legal, medical, architectural). Highly recommended for all service businesses.

**What is the difference between claims-made and occurrence-based?** Claims-made covers claims made during policy period; occurrence covers incidents that occurred during policy period. Claims-made is more common and affordable.

**Does professional indemnity cover intentional misconduct?** No. Only covers genuine errors, omissions, and negligence. Intentional fraud or criminal acts are excluded.

**Can I extend coverage after policy expires?** Yes, extended reporting period (ERP) available for 60-90 days after policy expiry, covering incidents during active policy period.

**How much coverage do I need?** Based on: contract values, potential claim amount, industry standards, and client requirements. We recommend minimum 50 lakhs for small businesses.

**Does it cover breach of confidentiality?** Yes, most professional indemnity policies cover breach of confidentiality as part of professional duty.
