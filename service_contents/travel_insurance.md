## Travel Insurance: Your Essential 2026 Guide for Safe Journeys

### Why Every Indian Traveller Needs Travel Insurance

With international tourism rebounding post-2025 and domestic travel surging across India, travel insurance protects your investment in vacations, business trips, and medical emergencies abroad. A single medical emergency overseas can cost ₹5-50 lakhs without coverage.

According to IRDAI 2026 data, the average medical evacuation cost from Southeast Asia to India is ₹3-5 lakhs, while emergency surgery in the USA can cost ₹50-80 lakhs. Travel insurance premiums typically cost just 1-3% of your total trip value, providing unlimited peace of mind.

### Types of Travel Insurance Coverage

1. **Individual Travel Insurance**: Single person coverage, ideal for solo travelers and business trips. Premium starts at ₹500/week.

2. **Family Travel Insurance**: Covers entire family (2 adults + 3 children typically) under one policy. Saves 30-40% compared to individual policies.

3. **Student Travel Insurance**: Specifically designed for international students. Covers tuition fees, medical expenses, and personal belongings. Premium ₹3,000-8,000 for 12-month coverage.

4. **Corporate Travel Insurance**: Group coverage for employees traveling on business. Bulk discounts for 10+ employees.

5. **Senior Citizen Travel Insurance**: Specialized coverage for travelers above 60 years with reduced medical exclusions.

### Claim Process: Step by Step

1. **Immediate Emergencies (24x7 helpline)**: Call the insurer's emergency number before receiving medical treatment. They provide hospital network details and direct admission arrangements.

2. **Cashless Hospitalization**: Present your policy details at network hospital. Insurer directly pays hospital bills up to coverage limit.

3. **Reimbursement Claims**: Pay upfront, collect all receipts and medical reports, submit within 30 days of return to India.

4. **Baggage Delay Claims**: File immediate report with airline. Submit delay receipt along with receipts for essential purchases made during delay.

5. **Trip Cancellation Claims**: Submit cancellation proof, booking receipts, and reason for cancellation (medical certificate for illness).

### Frequently Asked Questions

**How far in advance should I buy travel insurance?**

Ideally at the time of booking your trip. Some policies allow 14-21 days prior to travel. Emergency medical coverage begins immediately upon purchase. For Schengen visa, purchase must be made before visa application submission.

**Is travel insurance included with my credit card?**

Some premium cards offer basic coverage, but it often excludes pre-existing conditions, adventure activities, medical evacuation, and trip cancellation. Separate travel insurance is strongly recommended for comprehensive protection.

**Can I extend my trip if I buy travel insurance?**

Most policies allow extensions (with additional premium) before the return date. Extensions must be requested before the original policy period expires. Some insurers offer automatic extensions up to 7 days with advance notification.

**What about COVID-19 coverage?**

All 2026 travel insurance policies include COVID-19 medical coverage as standard. Quarantine coverage, trip cancellation due to COVID-19, and emergency hospitalization abroad are included. Some countries require negative COVID test results before allowing entry—check destination-specific requirements.

**Can I claim if my flight is delayed or cancelled?**

Yes. Most policies cover delays beyond 6-12 hours with compensation ranging from ₹5,000-25,000 depending on delay duration and policy terms. Full trip cancellation coverage pays up to 100% of prepaid trip cost.

**What is covered under baggage delay?**

Expenses for essential items (clothes, toiletries) while baggage is delayed. Typically ₹1,000-2,000 per day for up to 7 days, or actual expenses whichever is lower.

**Do I need insurance for domestic travel within India?**

Recommended but not mandatory. Domestic travel insurance covers trip cancellation, baggage loss, and personal accident. Premium starts at ₹200-500 per trip.

**How do I choose between cashless and reimbursement hospitals?**

Cashless hospitals are listed in your policy document and insurer's website. Always check the network hospital list for your destination before traveling. Keep all original receipts for reimbursement claims.

**Is there coverage for adventure activities like bungee jumping or skydiving?**

Standard policies exclude extreme sports. Add Adventure Sports Rider (₹1,000-3,000) covering bungee jumping, skydiving, scuba diving (up to 30m), and white water rafting.
