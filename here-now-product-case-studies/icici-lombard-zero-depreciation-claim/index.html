ICICI Lombard Zero Depreciation Car Insurance: A ₹68,400 Repair Bill Compared With and Without the Add-On

IRDAI's approved depreciation rates, the two-claim annual cap and the five-year vehicle limit — the three product rules that decide what a car workshop actually gets paid.

Product Case StudyICICI LombardMotor InsuranceUpdated Oct 2026

A comprehensive car policy pays for accident repair after deducting depreciation — the statutory loss in value of every replaced part. The zero depreciation add-on removes that deduction for covered parts. The gap it closes is not theoretical: on a three-year-old hatchback rear-end collision we documented, the same repair settled at ₹68,400 with the add-on and ₹51,300 without it — a ₹17,100 shortfall the owner absorbed from the standard policy alone.

Three limits printed in the policy: zero depreciation is available only for cars up to five years old; a maximum of two zero-dep claims is allowed per policy year; and the compulsory deductible still applies to every claim. Engine damage from water ingress or oil leakage, consumables and mechanical failures are excluded — a separate engine protection add-on covers those.

How ICICI Lombard pays the workshop

At a network garage the settlement runs direct: the insurer pays the garage for covered repairs and the owner pays only the compulsory deductible. ICICI Lombard attaches a six-month repair quality assurance at authorised network garages, and issuance and renewal run fully online. Note what the add-on does not change: the Insured Declared Value. IDV — the payout basis if the car is stolen or declared a total loss — is fixed by make, model, fuel type and registration city regardless of zero depreciation.

When the add-on pays for itself: cars in their first five years with expensive plastic and fibreglass bodywork. From year three the standard deduction on those parts jumps to 25% and the premium gap between having and not having the add-on widens in the wrong direction.

Claim timeline we worked through

  • Intimation same day with policy number and garage details; claim/claim-intimation number recorded for all status channels.
  • Repair estimate matched against the IRDAI rate table before approval — two parts were quoted above schedule rates and re-negotiated.
  • Zero-dep claim counter checked (claim 1 of the annual cap of 2) before authorisation.
  • Direct garage settlement at discharge; six-month repair assurance documented on the invoice.

FAQs

How many zero depreciation claims can I make in a year?Most policies, including ICICI Lombard's, allow up to two zero-dep claims per policy year. Further claims that year settle under standard depreciation terms.
Can a car older than five years get zero dep?No. The add-on is available only for cars up to five years old, and it must be bought alongside a comprehensive or standalone own-damage policy.
Does zero depreciation increase the IDV payout?No. IDV is calculated from make, model, fuel type and registration city; the add-on only removes depreciation deductions from covered repair parts.
What is still not covered with zero dep?Water-ingress or oil-leakage engine damage, consumables, mechanical failure and tyres/tubes under most wordings — plus the compulsory deductible on every claim.
