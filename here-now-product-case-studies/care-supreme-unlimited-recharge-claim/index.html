Care Supreme by Care Health: How Unlimited Recharge and the 50% Cumulative Bonus Played Out in a Two-Hospitalisation Year

A product-level look at Care Supreme — no room-rent cap, unlimited automatic recharge, guaranteed 50% cumulative bonus and the consumables gap — with the plan's published terms and Care Health's IRDAI claim record.

Product Case StudyCare HealthCare SupremeUpdated Oct 2026

Care Supreme is Care Health's flagship individual/floater indemnity plan, with sum insured from ₹5 lakh up to ₹6 crore. Two policyholders in one family hit the same ₹10 lakh sum insured twice in a single policy year — a planned surgery and, seven months later, an emergency admission. The plan terms, not the sum insured, decided the second claim: unlimited automatic recharge refilled the cover, and the cumulative bonus had already lifted it.

Why the second admission cost nothing extra: the Unlimited Automatic Recharge refilled the ₹10 lakh the same year — for a different illness as well as the same one — and the 50% cumulative bonus is guaranteed, not forfeited on claim, so the cover base itself was growing each renewal.
The catch worth reading twice: consumables are not in the base plan. The 68 IRDAI-listed non-payable items (gloves, masks, medicines bought at the hospital pharmacy, disposables) are paid only under the paid Claim Shield add-on. A settled claim can still leave a separate bill for these — we see it in almost every final settlement we review.

Care Health's claim record behind the plan

On IRDAI-published figures, Care Health settles 96.0% of claims (three-year weighted average as of FY 2024–25) and draws 42.9 complaints per 10,000 claims — above the recommended limit of 10, though fewer than several peers. Care Health reports pre-authorisation response times averaging about 2.5 hours for cashless treatment, final cashless settlement around five days, and reimbursement averaging 12 days from document submission. Its network covers 23,500+ hospitals, and under IRDAI's Cashless Everywhere mandate a non-network hospital registered under the Clinical Establishments Act can also be arranged cashless with advance notice.

How we worked the two claims

  • First claim: planned admission pre-auth filed 48 hours ahead; room category left uncapped per plan wording, so no proportionate deduction risk.
  • Second claim: recharge invoked explicitly on the claim form — many hospitals don't know it exists and quote the "exhausted" sum insured.
  • Consumables bill tracked separately and pushed through the Claim Shield add-on instead of being written off.
  • Both final settlements checked line-by-line against the discharge summary before acceptance.

FAQs

Does Care Supreme limit room rent?No. Room rent and ICU charges are both payable up to the sum insured, with no capping in the base plan.
Is the 50% cumulative bonus lost after a claim?No. Unlike a no-claim bonus, Care Supreme's cumulative bonus is guaranteed — 50% per year up to a 100% ceiling, unaffected by claims.
Are consumables covered in Care Supreme?Not in the base plan. The paid Claim Shield add-on covers the 68 listed non-payable items; the wider list comes via the separate Care Advanced add-on policy.
How many times does the sum insured restore?Unlimited times within a policy year, for the same illness as well as a different one, under the plan's Unlimited Automatic Recharge.
