## LIC Policies in India: A Practical Guide to Choosing, Managing, and Claiming

The Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) sells several kinds of life insurance and annuity products. The right way to compare them is to start with the financial need—income protection, long-term savings, a defined maturity benefit, or retirement income—and then read the specific policy terms. A product name or illustration alone is not enough to understand what is guaranteed, what depends on bonuses or investment performance, and what happens if premiums stop.

This guide is a map to LIC policy decisions and services. It does not rank current plans or predict returns. LIC changes products and servicing procedures; use LIC's current documents and your issued policy for plan-specific details.

### What kinds of LIC policies exist?

**Term insurance** provides life cover for a chosen period. A pure term plan is designed primarily to protect dependants if the insured dies during the term; it may not pay a maturity amount if the insured survives, unless the product says otherwise.

**Endowment and savings policies** combine life cover with a contract-based maturity or survival benefit. The premium is generally higher than pure term cover for the same life assured because the product has a savings component. Benefits can include guaranteed amounts and, where applicable, bonuses that are not guaranteed in advance.

**Money-back policies** may pay scheduled survival benefits during the term and a further benefit at maturity, under the policy's terms. Review how those payments change the remaining cover and what happens on death.

**Unit-linked insurance policies (ULIPs)** combine insurance with market-linked investment funds. The policyholder bears investment risk for the chosen funds, and charges, switching terms, lock-in conditions, and benefits matter. Compare the policy illustration and product documents; past fund performance does not assure future returns.

**Pension and annuity products** are intended to convert savings into retirement income under selected options. Check the annuity type, payment frequency, survivor benefits, purchase price, and whether the choice is reversible. Do not compare an annuity only by its first payment amount.

These broad categories are not substitutes for the product brochure and policy wording. Two plans in the same category can have different eligibility, benefits, charges, conditions, and servicing options.

### Match the policy to a real financial goal

Before comparing products, write down the goal, who depends on the insured person's income, how long that need lasts, and what other assets or cover exist. For income protection, estimate the financial gap dependants would face if the earner died. For a savings goal, compare the policy's cash flows with alternatives that have different liquidity, risk, and tax treatment. For retirement income, check whether the payment option protects a spouse or other beneficiary and what happens to capital after death.

Separate insurance from investment when it clarifies the decision. A policy that combines them can still suit a particular person, but compare its protection, flexibility, liquidity, and costs with buying cover and saving separately. Do not buy a plan solely because a salesperson highlights a tax benefit, bonus illustration, or projected maturity amount.

### Read the benefit illustration and policy schedule carefully

The illustration should distinguish guaranteed benefits from non-guaranteed benefits. Identify the premiums and their payment period, policy term, sum assured, survival and maturity benefits, death benefit, riders, and any conditions. If bonuses are illustrated, confirm whether the illustration labels them as non-guaranteed and what assumptions it uses. A projected figure is not a promised payout unless the contract identifies it as guaranteed.

The policy schedule is the record of the cover issued to you. Check the life assured, policyholder, nominee details, age, premium, payment frequency, commencement date, term, sum assured, riders, and any special terms. Compare it with the proposal form and the final offer. If an answer was entered incorrectly or a medical detail is missing, raise it promptly through LIC's official servicing channel and keep the correspondence.

### Disclose information accurately at proposal stage

Answer each question in the proposal form yourself where possible. Disclose medical history, tobacco or alcohol use, occupation, existing insurance, income, and other information the form asks for. If a question is unclear, ask the authorized channel to clarify it in writing before submission. Keep a copy of the signed proposal form, medical records provided, and all declarations.

Accurate disclosure protects both sides. If an insurer later raises non-disclosure, the proposal form and evidence of what was disclosed become central. Do not sign a blank form or accept a promise that information can safely be omitted because it is “not important.”

### Keep the policy in force and understand missed premiums

Save the premium due dates and use an official LIC payment channel. Keep receipts and check that payments are credited to the correct policy. If a premium is missed, read the policy's grace period and lapse provisions; exact rules vary by plan and contract. Contact LIC for a current revival quotation and the requirements that apply to your policy. Revival may require overdue premiums, interest, health evidence, or other underwriting, depending on the policy and LIC's decision.

Do not assume that a lapsed policy can be restored on the same terms or that a claim during a lapse will be payable. Ask LIC for written status, available revival options, amounts due, and the date risk cover would resume if revival is accepted. Use the current [LIC policy guidelines](https://licindia.in/en/web/guest/policy-guidelines-helpline) and [official forms page](https://licindia.in/en/web/guest/download-forms).

### Policy servicing: status, contact details, nomination, and documents

Use LIC's official [customer services and portal](https://www.licindia.in/en/web/guest/customer-services) to check available servicing options, policy status, and current requirements. Keep the registered mobile number, email, address, and bank details current through the approved process. If the digital portal does not support a request, contact the servicing branch using details from LIC's official website.

Review nomination details and update them after relevant family changes. Under Section 39 of the [Insurance Act, 1938](https://noc.irdai.gov.in/Content/docs/Insurance%20Act%2C1938%20-%20incorporating%20all%20amendments%20till%2020212021-08-12.pdf), nomination is made and recorded through the insurer's process, and the legal effect can depend on the facts and statutory provisions. A nomination should not be treated as a substitute for estate planning or legal advice in a disputed family situation. Keep confirmation that LIC registered the change.

Store the policy bond or digital policy, proposal form, receipts, benefit illustration, endorsements, and service requests in a place your family can access if needed. Tell a trusted family member where the documents are kept, but do not share portal passwords over chat or with unsolicited callers.

### Loan, surrender, or paid-up status: compare before choosing

If you need cash, ask LIC for written quotations for every option available under your specific policy. A policy loan, where allowed, may provide funds while keeping the contract in force, but interest accrues and unpaid amounts may reduce a later benefit. Surrender ends the policy and may produce less than the total premiums paid; the surrender value depends on the contract and timing. A paid-up option may reduce benefits while keeping a reduced policy in force, if the contract permits it.

Compare the amount received now, remaining life cover, future benefits, loan interest, taxes where applicable, and whether the policy can be restored later. Ask for figures in writing. Do not assume a loan, surrender, or paid-up value from an online calculator is exact until LIC confirms it for the policy number and date.

### How to file a LIC maturity or survival benefit claim

LIC may send a communication before a scheduled maturity or survival benefit, but keep your own record of the due date. Check the current requirements with the servicing branch or LIC portal. The process may require a claim or discharge form, policy details, identity confirmation, and bank mandate or NEFT details. Submit documents through an official branch or accepted digital channel and keep acknowledgement.

If the due date passes without payment, first confirm whether LIC has the current bank details and whether it needs a document or clarification. Use the policy number and written reference when contacting the branch. Do not assume a payment was made solely from a text message or a link; verify through the official LIC website or bank statement.

### How families can make a death claim

The nominee or other person entitled to claim should notify LIC through the servicing branch or official channel and ask for the current claim requirements for that policy. LIC's official [claims settlement requirements and forms](https://licindia.in/en/web/guest/download-forms) are the right starting point. Requirements can vary with the policy, duration, cause of death, and records available.

Families commonly need policy details, a death certificate, claimant identity and bank documents, and claim forms. LIC may request medical, employment, hospital, police, or other records depending on the circumstances. Ask for a complete written list, submit legible copies through the approved route, and retain originals unless LIC specifically requires them. An agent's informal checklist should not replace LIC's current request.

If a claim is delayed or declined, ask for the written status or decision and the exact reason. Compare it with the policy terms, proposal disclosures, and evidence. The family can raise a written grievance with LIC, then use the [IRDAI Bima Bharosa portal](https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in/) or consider the [Insurance Ombudsman](https://www.cioins.co.in/) if eligible. The [LIC claim rejection guide](/blog/lic-claim-rejection-appeal-guide-2026/) explains how to organize a challenge.

### Avoid common LIC policy mistakes

- Choosing a plan from a product name without reading guaranteed and non-guaranteed benefits.
- Treating an illustration, bonus estimate, or tax explanation as a contractual promise.
- Leaving proposal-form answers blank or relying on someone else to disclose medical history.
- Missing premiums and assuming the policy remains active without checking status.
- Surrendering before comparing the current surrender value with paid-up and loan options.
- Failing to update nomination, contact details, or bank information after changes.
- Trusting unsolicited calls, unofficial apps, or payment links that imitate LIC.
- Assuming a nominee automatically resolves every inheritance or ownership dispute.

## FAQ

**Q: Is LIC a government guarantee for every policy benefit?**
Do not treat a general description of LIC as a guarantee of every illustrated amount. Read the issued policy to identify contractual benefits, conditions, and any non-guaranteed elements, and use LIC's official product documents.

**Q: How can I check my LIC policy status?**
Use LIC's official customer portal or contact the servicing branch through contact details on LIC's website. Keep the policy number and verify the displayed status, premium due dates, and service information against your policy documents.

**Q: What happens if I stop paying premiums?**
The result depends on the policy terms, how many premiums were paid, and when payment stopped. Ask LIC for the current in-force status, revival terms, and any paid-up or surrender value before deciding what to do.

**Q: Is surrendering an LIC policy the same as taking a policy loan?**
No. Surrender ends the policy under its terms, while a loan—if available—creates an amount owed that may affect benefits. Request written, policy-specific quotations and compare the consequences before choosing.

**Q: Can a nominee file an LIC death claim?**
A nominee can notify LIC and submit a claim, but the insurer may need evidence of entitlement and other documents under the policy and applicable law. In a contested estate, get qualified legal advice rather than relying on a generic online answer.

**Q: Where should I get LIC claim forms?**
Use LIC's official forms and customer-service pages or ask the servicing branch for the current form and checklist for the exact claim. Avoid relying on old copies hosted by unofficial websites.
