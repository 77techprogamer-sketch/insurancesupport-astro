## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Can I buy health insurance for parents above 75?
**Star Red Carpet** allows entry up to 75. **Care Senior** up to 70. Beyond 75, options are limited — consider **super top-up on existing policy** or **group insurance via senior citizen associations**. We can check LIC's Varishtha Mediclaim (entry up to 80).

### 2. What if my parents have a claim in Year 1 — will premium jump?
**No**. Health insurance premiums don't increase based on claims (unlike motor). Premiums only increase with age (at renewal slabs: 60–65, 66–70, 71–75). NCB is **not** reduced for health claims in most modern plans.

### 3. Should I buy individual policies or family floater?
**Individual policies** for parents above 60. Family floaters share SI — one major hospitalization can exhaust cover for everyone. Individual policies give dedicated SI per parent.

### 4. Can I port my parents' existing policy to a better plan?
**Yes, IRDAI portability applies**. Waiting period credit transfers for completed years. We handle the entire porting process — typically 15–20 days. **Best time to port**: 30–45 days before renewal.

### 6. Are there any hidden exclusions in these senior citizen plans?
Standard exclusions across all: cosmetic surgery, dental (unless accident), infertility, war/nuclear, self-inflicted injury. **Plan-specific**: Some exclude joint replacement in year 1, or have cataract sub-limits. We provide exclusion checklist per plan.

### 7. What about OPD coverage for regular checkups?
Only **ICICI Lombard** (optional add-on) and **Aditya Birla** (chronic care program) offer meaningful OPD. Most senior plans focus on hospitalization. For OPD, consider a separate outpatient plan or employer coverage.

### 8. Can NRIs buy these for parents in Bangalore?
**Yes**, if proposer (you) is NRI and parents are residents. Premium paid from NRE/NRO account. Claim settlement in INR to Indian bank account. We've helped 40+ NRI clients.
