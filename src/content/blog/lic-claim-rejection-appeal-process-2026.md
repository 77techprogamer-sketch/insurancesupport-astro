# LIC Claim Rejection Appeal Process 2026 — Complete Bangalore Guide

## Why This Guide Exists

We've handled **200+ LIC claim rejections** for Bangalore families since 2020. The pattern is consistent: LIC rejects, families panic, deadlines are missed, money is lost. This guide changes that.

**Key reality**: LIC rejects ~12-15% of death claims annually (IRDAI 2024 data). But **60-70% of appealed rejections get overturned** when the process is followed correctly with proper documentation.

---

## The 4-Stage LIC Appeal Ladder

```
Stage 1: LIC Internal Grievance (0-15 days)
    ↓ (if rejected)
Stage 2: IRDAI IGMS / Bima Bharosa (15-30 days)
    ↓ (if rejected)
Stage 3: Insurance Ombudsman Bangalore (30-90 days)
    ↓ (if rejected)
Stage 4: Consumer Commission / Civil Court (90+ days)
```

**Critical**: Each stage has hard deadlines. Miss one, and you lose the right to escalate.

## Stage 1: LIC Internal Grievance (Days 0-15)

### When to File
- Within **15 days** of receiving rejection letter
- Rejection letter must state **specific clause + reason**

### Required Documents
1. **Original rejection letter** (keep envelope for postmark date)
2. **Policy bond** (original + 2 copies)
3. **Death certificate** (original + 2 attested copies)
4. **Claim form** (Form 380/381/382 as applicable)
5. **Nominee ID proof** (Aadhaar, PAN)
6. **Bank details** (cancelled cheque + passbook front page)
7. **Medical records** (if rejection is medical non-disclosure)
8. **Affidavit** (if nominee is minor / multiple nominees)

## Stage 2: IRDAI IGMS / Bima Bharosa (Days 15-45)

### When to Escalate
- LIC doesn't respond within **15 days**
- LIC rejects grievance
- Response is unsatisfactory

### Two Parallel Channels (Use BOTH)

#### Channel A: IRDAI IGMS (Integrated Grievance Management System)
```
Portal: https://igms.irda.gov.in/
Registration: Mobile OTP + email
Policy Type: "Life Insurance"
Insurer: "Life Insurance Corporation of India"
Grievance Category: "Claim Settlement"
Attach: All Stage 1 documents + LIC grievance acknowledgment
Tracking: IGMS Reference Number (format: IGMS/YYYY/XXXXXX)
```

**IRDAI Mandate**: Insurer must respond within **15 days** of IGMS registration.

**Profile**: 58M, software engineer, term plan ₹1Cr, died 2023
**Rejection**: LIC claimed non-disclosure of hypertension (2018)
**Defense**: Proposal form asked "any disease in last 5 years" — hypertension diagnosed 2016, but "controlled, no medication" noted by family doctor

### Case 2: ₹75L Money-Back — Suicide Allegation
**Profile**: 45M, businessman, Jeevan Anand, died 2022 (14 months post-policy)
**Rejection**: Suicide within 1 year (policy year 2 = exclusion applies?)
**Defense**: Policy year calculation — death was 13 months 15 days after commencement. Section 45: 3 years not completed but fraud not proven. Police FIR: "accidental fall."

**Outcome**: Divisional Office grievance — **settled in 11 days** (no escalation needed)

## Interest Calculation — What You're Owed

**IRDAI Regulation 8(6)**: Insurer pays **9% p.a. simple interest** from:
- Date of claim intimation (if documents complete)
- Date of last document submission (if documents incomplete initially)

**Example**: ₹50L claim, intimated Jan 1 2024, settled Oct 1 2024 (9 months)
Interest = 50,00,000 × 9% × (9/12) = **₹3,37,500**

## FAQ — LIC Appeal Specific

**Q: Can I appeal if policy is < 3 years?**
A: Yes. Section 45 doesn't bar appeal — it only limits "fraud" defense. Non-disclosure ≠ fraud unless proven intentional.

**Q: What if nominee is minor?**
A: Appoint guardian via court order or use Form 380 with guardian certificate. We handle this.

**Q: LIC says "file in court directly."**
A: They cannot deny Ombudsman jurisdiction for claims ≤ ₹1 crore. It's your statutory right.

**Q: How long does full process take?**
A: 3-6 months typical if escalated to Ombudsman. 12-24 months if Consumer Commission.

**Q: Do I need a lawyer?**
A: Not for Ombudsman. For Consumer Commission, recommended. We coordinate both.
