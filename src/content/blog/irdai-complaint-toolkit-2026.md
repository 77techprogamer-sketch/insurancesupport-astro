## Part 1: The 3-Track Resolution System (Choose Your Path)

| Track | Authority | Best For | Timeline | Cost | Binding? |
|-------|-----------|----------|----------|------|----------|
| **Track 1** | **IRDAI Bima Bharosa** | Initial pressure, documentation, regulator record | 15–30 days | Free | No (but creates record) |

**Our Strategy**: **File Track 1 + Track 2 simultaneously (Day 31)**. Track 1 creates regulatory pressure; Track 2 delivers binding outcome. Track 3 if Track 2 fails or claim >₹50L.

## Part 2: Eligibility Self-Check (2 Minutes)

### For Insurance Ombudsman (Track 2 — Best Path):

| # | Requirement | Yes? | No? → Action |
|---|-------------|------|--------------|
| 1 | Valid insurance policy (Life/Health/General)? | ☐ | Get policy copy first |
| 2 | Claim filed and **rejected/partially rejected**? | ☐ | Wait for rejection letter |
| 3 | Claim amount **≤ ₹50 lakh** (incl. expenses)? | ☐ | Use Consumer Commission instead |
| 4 | **Internal grievance** filed with insurer (GRO)? | ☐ | File GRO complaint → wait 30 days |
| 5 | **30+ days** since GRO complaint (no response / unsatisfactory)? | ☐ | Wait or file if insurer replied |
| 6 | **NOT pending** in court / consumer forum / arbitration? | ☐ | Withdraw other proceedings first |
| 7 | Within **1 year** of insurer's final rejection letter? | ☐ | Check exact date — file urgently |

**All 7 YES → You're eligible for Ombudsman. Proceed to Part 3.**

## Part 3: Document Assembly — The 20-Document Master Checklist

**Print this. Check off every item. 3 copies each.**

### A. Identity & Policy (Mandatory)
- [ ] **Form P-II** (Ombudsman complaint form) — download from `cioins.co.in`
- [ ] **Policy Schedule** — all pages, certified copy
- [ ] **Proposal Form** — all pages — **CRITICAL** for PED/non-disclosure cases
- [ ] **Rejection Letter** — both sides + **envelope with postmark**
- [ ] **Nominee/Legal Heir Documents** (death claims): Death cert, succession cert, nominee ID, relationship proof

### B. Grievance Trail (Proves Exhaustion)
- [ ] **GRO Complaint Email** — sent copy + delivery receipt
- [ ] **GRO Complaint Registered Post** — speed post receipt + tracking
- [ ] **GRO Response** (if received) OR **No-Response Proof** (tracking showing 30+ days)
- [ ] **Bima Bharosa Acknowledgement** (if filed) — complaint number
- [ ] **IGMS Acknowledgement** (if filed) — complaint number

### C. Medical / Claim Evidence (Health/Life)
- [ ] **Complete Medical Records** — chronological: discharge summary, prescriptions, all investigation reports, doctor's notes
- [ ] **Treating Doctor's Certificate** — on hospital letterhead, detailed justification — **MOST CRITICAL DOCUMENT**
- [ ] **Itemized Bills** — hospital + pharmacy + diagnostics, all originals
- [ ] **Payment Receipts** — proof of payment (if reimbursement)
- [ ] **Claim Form Submitted** — your copy with acknowledgement
- [ ] **Surveyor Report** — request from insurer under Regulation 8
- [ ] **FIR / MLC** — for accident/motor/medico-legal cases

### D. Legal & Procedural
- [ ] **Affidavit** — notarized on ₹100 stamp paper (format in Part 6)
- [ ] **Power of Attorney** — if representative filing (notarized)
- [ ] **IRDAI Circulars/Regulations** — 1-page printouts of cited regulations
- [ ] **Supreme Court/HC Precedents** — 1-page summaries of cited cases

## Part 4: Rejection-Type Evidence Matrix (What to Attach for YOUR Rejection)

| Rejection Reason | Mandatory Evidence | Winning Argument |
|------------------|-------------------|------------------|
| **Pre-Existing Disease (PED)** | Proposal form showing declaration; 3+ years hospital records; Treating doctor cert: unrelated; Policy age > waiting period | IRDAI Circular 126/2016: controlled 48 months ≠ PED; 8-year moratorium |
| **Non-Disclosure / Sec 45** | Policy >3 years old; Medical exam at inception; Standard rates (no loading); Agent knew/records available | Sec 45: after 3 years, only *proven fraud* voids policy (SC: *LIC v. Channabasamma*) |
| **Treatment Not Covered** | Treating doctor cert: standard of care; Medical guidelines (NCCN/ICMR); Policy exclusion wording | Contra proferentem: exclusions interpreted against insurer |
| **Cashless Denied** | Hospital denial letter; Treating doctor cert; IRDAI circular on network hospital obligation | Network hospital denial ≠ claim denial; insurer liable for deficiency |
| **Delay in Intimation** | ICU/emergency records; Family affidavit; Proof of document submission; Insurer's own delays | "Reasonable time" — not fixed days; courts consider circumstances |
| **Fraud Allegation** | All originals preserved; Lawyer engaged; Demand insurer's evidence | Criminal standard: beyond reasonable doubt; burden on insurer |
| **Policy Lapse** | Revival application; Back premiums + interest paid; DGH submitted; Special revival scheme | IRDAI revival rules; death during grace = claim payable |

## Part 7: Hearing Preparation — How to Win at Ombudsman

### Your Hearing File (12 Tabs, Chronological)

| Tab | Contents | Purpose |
|-----|----------|---------|
| **1** | Form P-II + Affidavit + POA | Procedural compliance |
| **2** | Policy Schedule + Proposal Form | Contract terms, declarations |
| **3** | Rejection Letter + Envelope | Rejection proof + date |
| **4** | GRO Complaint + Delivery Proof | Exhaustion of internal remedy |
| **5** | GRO Response / No-Response Proof | 30-day rule satisfied |
| **6** | Medical Records (Chronological) | Clinical evidence |
| **7** | **Treating Doctor Certificate** | **Most powerful evidence** |
| **8** | Bills + Payment Receipts | Quantum proof |
| **9** | Surveyor Report / FIR / MLC | Independent evidence |
| **10** | Nominee/Legal Heir Docs | Standing to claim |
| **11** | Regulations + Precedents (1-pagers) | Legal authority |
| **12** | Written Rejoinder to Insurer | Rebuttal of insurer's response |

### 5-Minute Oral Presentation Script

> "Hon'ble Ombudsman, I am [Name]. Policy No. [NO]. Claim No. [NO]. Amount ₹[AMOUNT].
>
> **Facts**: [2 sentences — e.g., "My father, aged 68, was hospitalized for cardiac surgery from DATE to DATE at HOSPITAL. Claim filed on DATE."]
>
> **Rejection**: Insurer rejected on [DATE] citing "[REASON]".
>
> **Why wrong**: [3 bullets — reference tabs]
> - Tab 2: Condition declared in proposal; waiting period completed on DATE
> - Tab 7: Treating cardiologist certifies standard of care, unrelated to declared condition
> - Tab 11: IRDAI Circular 126/2016 — controlled 48 months ≠ PED
>
> **Relief**: Full ₹[AMOUNT] + interest + costs.
>
> **Ready for questions.**"

### Hearing Day Checklist:
- [ ] Arrive 30 minutes early
- [ ] Bring original documents + 3 sets of copies
- [ ] Bring pen, notepad, water
- [ ] Dress formally (business attire)
- [ ] Switch phone to silent
- [ ] Address Ombudsman as "Hon'ble Ombudsman" or "Sir/Madam"
- [ ] Don't interrupt insurer
- [ ] Refer to tabs by number: "As per Tab 7..."

## Part 11: Appeal Escalation Roadmap

```
LEVEL 1: Internal Grievance (GRO)
         │
         ▼ 30 days no response / unsatisfactory
LEVEL 2: IRDAI Bima Bharosa + IGMS (Parallel)
         │
         ▼ 15 days no resolution
LEVEL 3: Insurance Ombudsman (BEST PATH ≤₹50L)
         │
         ▼ Award not paid / rejected recommendation
LEVEL 4: Execution Petition (Ombudsman) / Consumer Commission (>₹50L)
         │
         ▼ Complex legal issues / High value
LEVEL 5: Civil Court (Last Resort)
```

**Key Rule**: **Exhaust each level before next**. Skipping = dismissal.

## Part 14: Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Is Bima Bharosa the same as IGMS?
**No.** Bima Bharosa is the new unified portal. IGMS is legacy. **File on both** — they feed into same IRDAI monitoring.

### 2. Can I file Ombudsman complaint online?
**Most offices accept email**. Check `cioins.co.in` for current process. Bangalore accepts email.

### 3. What if I lose at Ombudsman?
**You can still go to Consumer Commission/Court**. Only the *award* is binding on insurer — *recommendation* is not binding on you.

### 4. How much does this cost?
**DIY**: ~₹500 (notarization, postage). **With us**: Free assessment, 15% of recovery (capped ₹5L) only on success.

### 5. Can I claim compensation for harassment?
**Yes**. Ombudsman/Consumer Commission routinely award **compensation + costs**. We include in every complaint.

### 6. My claim is ₹60L. Can I use Ombudsman?
**No — limit is ₹50L**. File in **District Consumer Commission** (≤₹1Cr). We handle both.

### 7. Do I need a lawyer for Ombudsman?
**No**. Designed for self-representation. We (non-lawyer advisors) represent clients regularly.

### 8. What if insurer doesn't pay after award?
**Execution petition** in same Ombudsman office. Insurer pays ₹5,000/day penalty. We've never had non-payment.
