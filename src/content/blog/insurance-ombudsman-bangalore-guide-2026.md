## Complaint Form — Field-by-Field Guide

### Section A: Complainant Details
- **Name**: As per policy
- **Address**: Current residential (for notices)
- **Mobile**: Active (SMS updates sent here)
- **Email**: Active (hearing links sent here)
- **PAN**: Mandatory

### Section B: Insurer Details
- **Name**: Exact (e.g., "Life Insurance Corporation of India")
- **Branch/Division**: Where policy serviced
- **Address**: Branch address from policy schedule

### Section C: Policy Details
- **Policy Number**: Exact
- **Type**: Life / Health / Motor / Other
- **Plan Name**: From policy schedule
- **Sum Assured / Sum Insured**: Exact amount
- **Date of Commencement**: From schedule

### Section D: Claim Details
- **Claim Type**: Death / Maturity / Health / Motor / Other
- **Date of Loss/Event**: Death date / Hospitalization date / Accident date
- **Date of Intimation**: When you informed insurer
- **Claim Amount**: What you're claiming
- **Amount Paid**: If partial (usually 0)

### Section E: Grievance History (CRITICAL)
- **Date of Grievance to Insurer**: Exact date
- **Mode**: Online / Letter / Email / Walk-in
- **Acknowledgment Number**: From insurer
- **Insurer Response**: Rejected / No response / Unsatisfactory
- **Response Date**: When received

### Section F: Grounds of Complaint (THE CORE)
**Write in numbered paragraphs**:

```
1. REJECTION NOT SUSTAINABLE — SECTION 45
   Policy in force > 3 years. Insurer alleges non-disclosure of [condition].
   Section 45(1) Insurance Act 1938: After 3 years, policy cannot be questioned
   on ground of misstatement unless fraud proven. Insurer has not proven fraud.
   
2. MATERIALITY NOT ESTABLISHED
   Alleged non-disclosure: [condition]. Current claim: [unrelated condition].
   No causal link. IRDAI Master Circular: Materiality = influence on acceptance
   of risk OR premium. Insurer has not shown materiality.
   
3. WAITING PERIOD WRONGLY APPLIED
   Condition [X] not in policy's "Specific Disease List." Only initial 30-day
   waiting period applies. Policy inception: [Date]. Claim: [Date]. 30 days passed.
   
4. EXCLUSION CLAUSE AMBIGUOUS
   Exclusion: "[Vague wording]." IRDAI Standardization Circular 2022: Exclusions
   must be "clear, unambiguous, prominently displayed." This exclusion fails.
   
5. IRDAI REGULATIONS VIOLATED
   - Regulation 8(6): Interest @ 9% from claim intimation not paid
   - Regulation 14: Claim settled beyond 30 days without justification
   - Cashless Regulation 2026: Decision not communicated within 2 hours
```

### Section G: Relief Sought
Be specific:
```
a) Direct insurer to pay claim amount ₹[X] with interest @ 9% p.a. from [date]
b) Award compensation for mental agony ₹[Y]
c) Award litigation expenses ₹[Z]
d) Direct insurer to issue policy servicing compliance
```

## Winning Strategies — From 150+ Bangalore Cases

### 1. Section 45 is Your Strongest Weapon (Life + Health > 3 Years)

**The Law**: Insurance Act 1938, Section 45:
> "No policy of life insurance shall be called in question on any ground whatsoever after the expiry of three years from the date of the policy..."

**What It Means**:
- Policy > 3 years = **insurer MUST prove fraud**
- Non-disclosure ≠ fraud (fraud = intentional deception)
- Burden of proof: **ENTIRELY ON INSURER**

**How We Win**:
1. Get proposal form via RTI if insurer won't share
2. Show ambiguity in questions
3. Prove condition was known/controlled, not hidden
4. Cite: *LIC v. G.M. Channabasamma* (SC) — mere non-disclosure ≠ fraud

---

### 2. IRDAI Regulations Are Enforceable at Ombudsman

**Key Regulations to Cite**:

| Regulation | Provision | How We Use It |
|------------|-----------|---------------|
| **Protection of Policyholders' Interests, 2017** | Claim settlement in 30 days | Delay = interest @ 9% + compensation |
| **Reg 8(6)** | Interest from intimation date | Calculate exact interest owed |
| **Reg 14** | Claim repudiation must state reasons | Vague rejection = regulatory violation |
| **Cashless Claims Reg 2026** | 30 min emergency / 2 hr planned | TPA delay = penalty claim |
| **Standardization Circular 2022** | Exclusions must be clear | Ambiguous exclusion = unenforceable |

---

### 3. Medical Evidence > Insurer's Medical Officer

**Insurer's MO**: Never examined patient, reads papers only
**Your Doctor**: Treated patient, knows clinical reality

**Winning Formula**:
1. Treating doctor's detailed letter (template in our LIC/ICICI guides)
2. Specialist opinion if complex (cardiologist, oncologist, neurologist)
3. **Cross-examination readiness**: We prepare doctor for Ombudsman questions
4. **Medical literature**: ICMR/NCCN guidelines supporting treatment

---

### 4. Portability Credit = Waiting Period Credit

**IRDAI Portability Guidelines 2020**:
> "The waiting period served under the previous policy shall be credited..."

**How We Win**:
1. Get portability certificate from previous insurer (mandatory)
2. Calculate exact days covered
3. Show new insurer's waiting period already satisfied
4. **Bangalore Ombudsman 2024**: Upheld portability credit for 2-year PED wait

## FAQ — Bangalore Ombudsman

**Q: Can I file if insurer hasn't responded to grievance in 30 days?**
A: **Yes**. 30-day silence = deemed rejection. File with proof of grievance submission.

**Q: What if claim is ₹1.2 crore?**
A: Ombudsman limit is ₹1 crore. File in **Consumer Commission** directly. Or claim ₹1 crore at Ombudsman, balance at Commission.

**Q: Do I need a lawyer?**
A: **Not mandatory**. But for > ₹10L, insurer sends lawyer. We represent you (non-lawyer authorized rep allowed).

**Q: How long does the whole process take?**
A: **3-5 months** typical. Fastest: 2 months (grievance level settlement). Slowest: 8 months (complex medical).

**Q: Can I attend hearing virtually?**
A: **Yes, default is virtual** (Zoom/Teams). Physical only if you request.

**Q: What if insurer doesn't pay after Ombudsman order?**
A: Execution petition at Ombudsman → recovery certificate → district collector attachment. We handle this.

**Q: Can I claim mental agony compensation?**
