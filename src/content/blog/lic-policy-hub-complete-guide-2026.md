# LIC Policy Hub: Complete 2026 Guide to Life Insurance Corporation of India

## Understanding LIC Policy Basics — What Is Life Insurance Corporation of India?

Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) is India's leading life insurance company, formed in 1956. With over 20 crore policyholders, LIC offers a wide range of insurance plans under categories including:

- **Savings-linked endowment plans** (Jeevan Anand, Jeevan Shanti, Jeevan Lakshya)
- **Term insurance** (LIC Tech Term, LIC e-Term)
- **Money-back plans** (Jeevan Tarang, Jeevan Surabhi)
- **Child plans** (LIC Children's Career Plan, Amrutbaal)
- **Pension plans** (Jeevan Shanti Immediate Annuity)
- **Health riders and add-ons**

## Frequently Asked Questions: LIC Policies

### Q: What is the process to check LIC policy status online?
A: Visit the LIC customer portal or use the LIC app to track your **LIC policy status check online**. Enter your registered **policy number** to view active status, due dates, and surrender value.

### Q: How to download LIC policy bond without login?
A: While the standard process requires login, we can help you obtain duplicate bonds using your **LIC policy details by policy number** along with KYC documentation.

### Q: What is the LIC policy loan interest rate for 2026?
A: Current **LIC policy loan interest rate** typically ranges between 9-10% per annum. The loan is available for policies that have completed the basic survival period (usually 3 full years of premium payment).

### Q: Can I surrender my LIC policy online?
A: **Online LIC policy surrender** is possible through the LIC portal if your policy is fully paid-up and eligible. For partially paid policies, visit the branch physically. We assist with calculating surrender value beforehand.

### Q: What are the best LIC plans for investment in 2026?
A: Based on bonus rates, the best LIC plans for 2026 include **Jeevan Anand** (endowment), **Jeevan Shanti** (pension), **Jeevan Lakshya** (money back), **Jeevan Labh** (children's), and **Tech Term** (pure protection).

### Q: What is the age limit for buying LIC policies?
A: **LIC policy age limit** varies by plan: child plans accept newborns from day one, while adult plans typically range from 21-70 years at entry.

### Q: How to change address in LIC policy online?
A: For **LIC policy address change online**, login to the LIC customer portal, navigate to profile update, and submit documents (Aadhaar, passport size photo, address proof). For assistance, see your local LIC agent.

### Q: What is Amrutbaal LIC policy and its benefits?
A: **Amrutbaal LIC policy** is LIC's children's savings plan providing guaranteed returns with comprehensive coverage. It provides for daughter/son care benefits and maturity payouts at age 21 for girl children and 25 for boys.
