```

   → TPA settles with hospital directly
```

## Rejection Deep-Dive — Top 3 with Defenses

### 1. PED Non-Disclosure (38% of rejections)

**Typical Scenario**: Policyholder had hypertension/diabetes/thyroid before policy. Didn't declare or declared incompletely.

**Care Health's Argument**: "Material non-disclosure — would have charged higher premium / applied waiting period / rejected proposal."

**Your Defense Arsenal**:

**A. Section 45 (Insurance Act 1938)**
- Policy > 3 years = **fraud must be proven**, not presumed
- Care Health must prove: (a) non-disclosure, (b) materiality, (c) fraudulent intent
- **Burden shifts to insurer** after 3 years

**B. Proposal Form Ambiguity**
- "Any disease in last 5 years?" vs "Any treatment in last 5 years?"
- Many proposals use vague language — courts favor policyholder
- **RTI to Care Health** for your proposal form copy

**C. Medical Evidence**
- Doctor certificate: "Condition controlled, no treatment change in 3 years"
- Pharmacy bills showing stable medication
- No hospitalization for alleged PED in policy period

**Bangalore Precedent**: Ombudsman Bangalore 2024 — Care Health rejection overturned for thyroid non-disclosure. Policy 4 years old. "Thyroid not considered material to gallbladder surgery."

---

### 2. Waiting Period Not Completed (22% of rejections)

**Types of Waiting Periods**:

| Waiting Period | Duration | Applies To |
|----------------|----------|------------|
| **Initial** | 30 days | All illnesses (except accident) |
| **Specific Disease** | 2 years | Cataract, hernia, stones, joint replacement, etc. |
| **PED** | 2-4 years | Pre-existing conditions declared |
| **Maternity** | 2-4 years | Delivery, newborn cover |

**Defense Strategies**:

**A. Policy Inception Date Audit**
- Many policies backdated to proposal date, not payment date
- Check: Policy schedule "Date of Commencement" vs First premium date
- **Gap = free coverage days**

**B. Portability Credit**
- Ported from another insurer? **Waiting period credit transfers**
- Care Health must honor: "Credit for period covered under previous policy"
- **Get portability certificate** from previous insurer

**C. Disease-Specific vs General**
- "Specific disease waiting period" only applies to LISTED diseases
- If your condition NOT in list → initial 30 days only
- **Demand the specific disease list** from policy wording

**Bangalore Case**: 45M, Care Advantage, kidney stone surgery at 14 months. Rejection: "2-year specific disease waiting period." Defense: Kidney stone NOT in policy's specific disease list (only: cataract, hernia, piles, fistula, sinus, tonsils, adenoids, joint replacement). **Overturned at IGMS in 12 days.**

---

### 3. Exclusion Clause Triggered (15% of rejections)

**Common Care Health Exclusions**:

| Exclusion | Wording Trap | Defense |
|-----------|--------------|---------|
| **Cosmetic/Plastic Surgery** | "Except necessitated by accident/illness" | Burns, reconstructive post-cancer, trauma = covered |
| **Dental Treatment** | "Except requiring hospitalization" | Impacted wisdom tooth + hospitalization = covered |
| **Fertility/IVF** | Absolute in most plans | Check "Care Joy" rider — separate maternity plan |
| **Obesity/Bariatric** | BMI > 35 + comorbidities | If medically necessary (diabetes, apnea) — argue |
| **Mental Health** | Many plans exclude | **IRDAI 2022 mandate**: Mental health = physical health. Challenge. |
| **HIV/AIDS** | Absolute | Check plan — some newer plans cover |
| **Self-inflicted/Suicide** | 1 year exclusion | Section 45 after 3 years, accident vs intent |

**Key Weapon**: **IRDAI Standardization Circular 2022** — Exclusions must be "clear, unambiguous, and prominently displayed." Vague exclusions = unenforceable.

## FAQ — Care Health Claims

**Q: Care Health says "waiting period not completed" but I ported from another insurer.**
A: **Portability credit is mandatory**. Get portability certificate from previous insurer. File grievance quoting IRDAI Portability Guidelines 2020.

**Q: My policy is Care Senior — 20% co-pay. Can I avoid it?**
A: No, co-pay is contractual. But fight **denial** first. Co-pay applies only to approved amount.

**Q: Care Health cashless denied at non-network hospital. Emergency?**
A: If genuine emergency (life/limb threat) AND nearest network > 15km → emergency cashless applies. Get emergency certificate from treating doctor.

**Q: How long does Care Health Ombudsman case take?**
A: 4-6 months typical. First hearing 30-45 days. Decision 15 days post-hearing.

**Q: Care Health rejected mental health claim.**
A: **Challenge it**. IRDAI 2022 mandate: mental illness = physical illness. Many exclusions now unenforceable. File IGMS + Ombudsman.
