## Understanding Health Insurance Basics

Health insurance protects your family from massive medical expenses. In India, medical inflation is rising at 12-15% annually — a single day in a private ICU costs ₹25,000-50,000.

### Coverage Options Covered Here:

| Plan Type | Key Information |
|---|---|
| **Family Floater** | Covers entire family under one sum insured |
| **Senior Citizen Plans** | Special coverage for age 60+ with reduced exclusions |
| **Super Top-Up** | Additional coverage above existing policy |
| **Critical Illness** | Lump-sum payout on diagnosis of specified illnesses |

## Frequently Asked Questions: Health Insurance

### Q: What is the waiting period for health insurance?
A: Most policies have a **30-45 day initial waiting period**, plus **2-4 year waiting periods** for specific conditions (cataract, hernia, joint replacement, pre-existing diseases).

### Q: Can I get cashless treatment with health insurance?
A: Yes, with cashless health insurance you can use **over 6000+ network hospitals** directly — no upfront payment needed.

### Q: How much health insurance coverage do I need?
A: Experts recommend **10-15 lakhs minimum for families**, plus a **Super Top-Up** of 20-30 lakhs for high-cost procedures.

### Q: Are pre-existing diseases covered in 2026?
A: Under **IRDAI standardized rules**, pre-existing conditions are covered after **4 years** of continuous coverage (reduced from 8 years under new guidelines).

### Q: How to file a health insurance portability request?
A: Submit Form PORT-REQ to your new insurer with 30 days of medical records. **Cashless claim pre-authorization** can continue during the transition period.

### Q: What is the health insurance claim process for cashless treatment?
A: The **cashless claim pre-authorization** must be submitted 48 hours before planned admission via TPA. For emergencies, intimation must be given within 48 hours of admission.
