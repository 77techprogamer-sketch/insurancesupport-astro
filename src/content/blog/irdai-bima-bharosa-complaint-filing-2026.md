# IRDAI Bima Bharosa Complaint Filing 2026 — Complete Step-by-Step Guide

## Two Platforms — One Regulator

| Platform | Launched | Best For | Status |
|----------|----------|----------|--------|
| **IGMS** (Integrated Grievance Management System) | 2011 | Formal regulatory complaints, legal record | **Mature, stable** |
| **Bima Bharosa** | 2025 | User-friendly, real-time tracking, video hearings | **New, improving** |

**Use BOTH in parallel** — they feed the same IRDAI database but have different strengths.

---

## When to File IRDAI Complaint

### Mandatory Pre-Condition
**You MUST file insurer's internal grievance FIRST**:
- File with insurer (portal/email/letter)
- Wait **15 days** for response
- If rejected OR no response → IRDAI complaint eligible

### Ideal Timing
- **Day 0**: Insurer rejects claim / grievance
- **Day 1**: File IGMS + Bima Bharosa (parallel)
- **Day 1-15**: IRDAI forwards to insurer (15-day response mandate)
- **Day 15-30**: Insurer responds / IRDAI follows up
- **Day 30+**: If unsatisfactory → Ombudsman / Consumer Commission

## IGMS — The Veteran Platform

### Access
```
Portal: https://igms.irda.gov.in/
Mobile: No dedicated app (browser only)
Languages: English, Hindi
```

### Registration
1. Click "Register as Complainant"
2. Enter: Name, Mobile, Email, PAN
3. OTP verification (mobile + email)
4. Login credentials created

### Filing Process — Step by Step

```
1. LOGIN → "File New Complaint"
2. SELECT COMPLAINT TYPE:
   - Life Insurance
   - General Insurance  
   - Health Insurance
   - Reinsurance (rare)
3. SELECT INSURER:
   - Dropdown: All IRDAI-registered insurers
   - Type to search (e.g., "Life Insurance Corporation")
4. ENTER POLICY DETAILS:
   - Policy Number (mandatory)
   - Policy Type (Term/Endowment/ULIP/Health/Motor/etc.)
   - Sum Assured / Sum Insured
5. ENTER CLAIM DETAILS:
   - Claim Intimation Date
   - Claim Number (if any)
   - Claim Amount
   - Amount Paid (usually 0)
6. GRIEVANCE CATEGORY (Critical — routes to right IRDAI team):
   - Claim Settlement → "Repudiation/Rejection"
   - Claim Settlement → "Delay in Settlement"
   - Claim Settlement → "Partial Settlement"
   - Policy Servicing → "Non-receipt of Policy"
   - Policy Servicing → "Premium Related"
   - Others → "Mis-selling"
   - Others → "Fraud"
7. GRIEVANCE DESCRIPTION (The Core — 4000 chars max):
   [Use our template below]
8. UPLOAD DOCUMENTS (Max 5MB each, PDF/JPG):
   - Policy schedule
   - Rejection letter
   - Insurer grievance acknowledgment
   - Medical documents (if health)
   - Any correspondence
9. SUBMIT → Get IGMS Reference: IGMS/YYYY/XXXXXX
```

### IGMS Complaint Description Template

```
Policy No: [XXXXXXXXX]
Insurer: [Exact Name]
Claim No: [XXXXXXXXX]
Date of Rejection: [DD/MM/YYYY]
Insurer Grievance Ref: [Number] dated [Date] (or "No response in 15 days")

GROUNDS FOR COMPLAINT:

1. VIOLATION OF IRDAI (PROTECTION OF POLICYHOLDERS' INTERESTS) REGULATIONS, 2017
   Regulation 8(6): Insurer liable to pay interest @ 9% p.a. from date of intimation
   of claim till date of payment. Claim intimated on [Date]. No interest paid.
   
   Regulation 14: Claim repudiation must communicate grounds with specific policy
   clause references. Rejection letter dated [Date] cites "[quote reason]" without
   clause reference.

2. SECTION 45, INSURANCE ACT 1938 — POLICY > 3 YEARS
   Policy commenced [Date]. Completed [X] years. Insurer alleges non-disclosure of
   [condition]. Section 45: After 3 years, policy cannot be questioned unless fraud
   proven. Insurer has not proven fraudulent intent. Mere non-disclosure ≠ fraud.
   (Ref: LIC v. G.M. Channabasamma, Supreme Court)

3. MATERIALITY NOT ESTABLISHED
   Alleged non-disclosure: [condition]. Current claim: [unrelated condition].
   No causal link. IRDAI Master Circular: Materiality = would have influenced
   acceptance of risk OR premium. Insurer has not demonstrated materiality.

4. WAITING PERIOD MISAPPLIED (if applicable)
   Condition [X] not in policy's Specific Disease List (Annexure). Only 30-day
   initial waiting period applies. Policy inception [Date] → Claim [Date] = >30 days.

5. CASHLESS REGULATIONS VIOLATED (if health cashless)
   IRDAI/Reg/13/2026: Emergency decision 30 min, Planned 2 hours.
   TPA [Name] took [X] hours. No written reasons per Regulation 4.5.

RELIEF SOUGHT:
a) Direct insurer to settle claim of ₹[Amount] with interest @ 9% p.a. from [intimation date]
b) Penalty for regulatory violations
c) Compensation for mental agony ₹[Amount]
d) Litigation expenses ₹[Amount]

DOCUMENTS ATTACHED: [List all]
```

### IGMS Tracking
- **Dashboard**: Shows status — "Forwarded to Insurer" / "Insurer Responded" / "Closed"
- **Insurer Response Time**: 15 days mandated (auto-escalates to IRDAI if missed)
- **Your Reply**: You can respond to insurer's reply within 7 days
- **Closure**: IRDAI closes if satisfied OR you escalate to Ombudsman

## Parallel Filing Strategy — Do Both

### Why File Both?
1. **IGMS**: Legal record, formal regulatory pressure, established process
2. **Bima Bharosa**: Better UX, video hearing, real-time chat, direct Ombudsman link
3. **Same IRDAI backend** — no conflict, dual pressure

### Execution
```
DAY 1 (Morning):
☐ File IGMS complaint (detailed, regulation-heavy)
☐ File Bima Bharosa complaint (same grounds, voice-to-text for speed)
☐ Save both reference numbers

DAY 1 (Evening):
☐ Email insurer nodal officer with both reference numbers
☐ Subject: "IRDAI Complaints Filed — IGMS: [Ref] | Bima Bharosa: [Ref]"

DAY 15:
☐ Check both platforms for insurer response
☐ If no response → IRDAI auto-escalates (both platforms)
☐ If response received → Analyze, reply on both platforms

DAY 30:
☐ If unsatisfactory → Click "Escalate to Ombudsman" on Bima Bharosa
☐ File Ombudsman complaint (see our Ombudsman guide)
```

## Insurer Response — What to Expect

### Typical Insurer Responses (in order of frequency)

| Response | Meaning | Your Counter |
|----------|---------|--------------|
| **"Under process"** | Buying time | Demand specific timeline, cite Regulation 14 |
| **"Documents insufficient"** | Standard delay tactic | List what was submitted, demand specific missing doc |
| **"Rejection upheld"** | Formal denial | Reply with point-by-point rebuttal + regulations |
| **"Partial settlement offered"** | Negotiation | Calculate full entitlement, reject if lowball |
| **"Refer to Ombudsman"** | They want you to go | Perfect — you were going anyway, now with IRDAI record |

### How to Reply on IGMS
```
Login → My Complaints → Select → "Submit Reply"
- Address each insurer point
- Cite regulations by number
- Attach new evidence if any
- Keep professional, factual
```

### How to Reply on Bima Bharosa
```
App → My Complaints → Select → "Respond"
- Voice-to-text for detailed reply
- Photo upload for new docs
- Chat with IRDAI officer for guidance
```

## Escalation Matrix — When IRDAI Isn't Enough

```
LEVEL 1: INSURER INTERNAL GRIEVANCE
    ↓ 15 days no response / rejection

LEVEL 2: IRDAI IGMS + BIMA BHAROSA (PARALLEL)
    ↓ 30 days / unsatisfactory response

LEVEL 3: INSURANCE OMBUDSMAN
    Jurisdiction: Based on insurer branch / policyholder residence
    Limit: ₹1 crore
    Timeline: 3-5 months
    ↓ If claim > ₹1 crore OR unfavorable

LEVEL 4: CONSUMER COMMISSION
    District: ≤ ₹1 crore
    State: ₹1-10 crore  
    National: > ₹10 crore
    Timeline: 1-3 years
    ↓ If legal complexity

LEVEL 5: HIGH COURT / SUPREME COURT
    Only for points of law / public interest
    Timeline: 3-10 years
```

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Consequence | Fix |
|---------|-------------|-----|

| Wrong grievance category | Routed to wrong team | Use "Claim Settlement → Repudiation" for rejections |

## FAQ — IRDAI Bima Bharosa / IGMS

**Q: Can I file on Bima Bharosa if I don't have a smartphone?**
A: Yes — portal at https://bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in/ works on any browser.

**Q: Is there a fee for IRDAI complaint?**
A: **Zero**. No filing fee, no court fee.

**Q: What if insurer settles after I file IRDAI complaint?**
A: **Withdraw complaint** on both platforms (one-click). Settlement stands.

**Q: Can I file for someone else (parent/spouse)?**
A: Yes — with Power of Attorney or as authorized representative. Bima Bharosa has "File for Family" feature.

**Q: What if insurer doesn't respond to IRDAI in 15 days?**
A: IRDAI issues **show-cause notice** → penalty proceedings. We've seen this work.

**Q: Bima Bharosa video hearing — do I need a lawyer?**
A: No. Authorized representative allowed. We represent clients regularly.

**Q: Can I file Bima Bharosa in Kannada?**
A: **Yes** — full Kannada interface, voice-to-text in Kannada, hearing in Kannada.

**Q: What's the difference between "Grievance" and "Complaint" on these platforms?**
A: Same thing. "Grievance" = insurer level. "Complaint" = IRDAI level.
