Insurer rejected citing age and pre-existing disease. We used IRDAI's senior citizen protections and got full claim paid.

Summary

This account and its amounts are reported by Insurance Support and have not been independently verified. Details are selective, and the outcome of another claim may differ.

A 72-year-old retired banker's claim for hospitalization was rejected by Star Health citing pre-existing hypertension and age-related exclusions. The policy had a 2-year waiting period that was completed. We proved the condition was declared at proposal and the waiting period was satisfied. Full 22 lakh approved within 38 days.

How We Solved It

S Situation

72-year-old retired banker from Jayanagar hospitalized for cardiac issue. Policy active for 4 years — waiting period completed. Star Health rejected citing 'pre-existing hypertension' and 'age-related risk' despite hypertension being declared at proposal.

T Task

Prove the pre-existing condition was declared and waiting period satisfied. Overturn age-based rejection which is prohibited by IRDAI.

A Action

  1. 1.Retrieved original proposal form — hypertension declared in medical history
  2. 2.Confirmed 4-year policy tenure — 2-year waiting period completed 2 years ago
  3. 3.Cited IRDAI Health Insurance Regulations 2016: no age-based claim rejection after waiting period
  4. 4.Obtained cardiologist's letter confirming current issue unrelated to declared hypertension
  5. 5.Filed appeal with GRO referencing IRDAI circular on senior citizen protections

R Result

Star Health GRO approved full 22,00,000 within 38 days. Reversal letter acknowledged waiting period was satisfied and age cannot be a rejection ground post-waiting-period.

Lessons Learned

Lessons Learned

  • Waiting period completion makes pre-existing conditions coverable
  • IRDAI prohibits age-based claim rejection after waiting period
  • Declared conditions at proposal = covered after waiting period
  • Senior citizen policies have specific regulatory protections
