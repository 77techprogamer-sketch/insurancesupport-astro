## Part B — Review-Request Message Templates

> **Rule:** Ask for reviews only from genuinely satisfied clients, *after* the job is done well. Never incentivize with money, gifts, or discounts.

### B1. WhatsApp review request (English — most common for phone contact)
```
Hi [Client Name],

Thank you so much for trusting me with your [claim recovery / insurance policy] —
I'm really glad we could [resolve your claim / you found the right cover].

If you're happy with how things went, would you mind leaving a quick review?
It genuinely helps other families in Bengaluru find trustworthy insurance help.

Google review (2 min): [paste your GBP short review link]

Thank you again — Hari Kotian
Insurance Support | IRDAI Reg. 0149161D | 25+ years
```

### B2. WhatsApp review request — short version (for quick replies)
```
Hi [Name], glad we could help you [win / complete] the [claim / policy]!

If you have a moment, a short Google review would mean a lot to me and helps
other families too. Here's the link: [GBP link]

Thanks! — Hari, Insurance Support
```

### B6. How to make the review link
- In GBP (business.google.com), go to the profile → **Ask for reviews** → **Share review form** → copy the short link that starts with `https://g.page/r/.../review`.
- Use that exact link in all templates above.

### B7. Handling a negative review (do this, don't ignore)
```

of what we aim for. I'd like to understand what went wrong and make it right.
Please reply here or call +91 99866 34506 so we can resolve this for you.

— Hari Kotian
```
- Then take the conversation offline, fix it, and circle back. Never argue publicly.
