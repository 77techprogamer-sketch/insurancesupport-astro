ICICI Lombard Travel Insurance Claim 2026: Delay, Baggage & Medical Documents

A travel claim is an annexure exercise — the right certificate from the airline, filed with stamped scans and bank details, is the whole game.

Travel insurance is bought in ninety seconds and claimed, if at all, in the worst week of a trip — a missed connection at Frankfurt, a suitcase that never leaves Delhi, a hospital admission in Bangkok. ICICI Lombard's travel claim machinery is document-driven and annexure-heavy: every cover type under the policy carries its own annexure in the wordings, its own proof requirement, and its own set of carve-outs. This piece walks the claim path cover by cover — flight delay, cancellation, baggage loss, baggage delay, medical, personal accident — with the documents ICICI Lombard's own claim forms ask for, the intake details that are non-negotiable (bank EFT particulars, scanned originals stamped for insurer use), and the escalation route if a travel claim stalls.

What a travel policy actually claims against

The claim form annexure map is the fastest route to understanding the product. ICICI Lombard's travel wordings organise covers into two families: indemnity covers (you get reimbursed for a proven financial loss, up to limits, minus depreciation and exclusions) and benefit covers (a fixed amount becomes payable when a defined event happens — a delay crossing its threshold, a baggage mishap confirmed by the carrier).

The organising principle: a benefit cover wants a certificate — a document from the airline, airport or police stating what happened — while an indemnity cover wants money trail — invoices proving what you owned or paid, and evidence that the loss landed on you rather than on the carrier's own liability channel.

Flight delay and cancellation: the benefit-cover dance

Delayed-flight claims fail for two predictable reasons. First, the delay does not meet the threshold or the cause sits inside the exclusion table: wordings annexures for common-carrier delay routinely carve out equipment failure and operational snags already diagnosed before departure, clearance-related holds, crew scheduling problems, and civil unrest or major travel events at either end of the route — the qualifying-versus-carve-out list is printed in the annexure and differs by edition, so read the one bound with your policy. Second, the traveller files without the carrier's own statement: ICICI Lombard's claim documents require the confirmation or correspondence from the common carrier stating the circumstances causing the delay — a screenshot of the airport board is supporting evidence, not the primary document. Airline delay certificates are issued on request at the airport service desk or through the airline's website form; get it before you leave the terminal, because retro-requests weeks later are slower than the claim itself.

The certificate from the carrier is the hinge — everything else is identification and payout mechanics.

Intimation timing matters across travel wordings: notify the insurer as soon as the event is known (and, where the wording says so, before the policy expires), because late intimation hands the insurer a legitimate query to sit on. The claim form itself carries the declaration language — filing with knowledge of inaccuracy exposes the claim to repudiation under the fraud clause, so submit exactly what happened, dated exactly as it happened.

Baggage claims: PIR first, everything else second

Checked-baggage loss begins at the airline's mishandling desk, not the insurer's: file the Property Irregularity Report before leaving the arrivals hall and keep the reference number. The PIR (or its successor in the airline's app) is the primary evidence for Cover 11's loss-of-baggage indemnity and for the electronic-equipment extension where a laptop or camera inside the bag is claimed — for contents, invoices or warranty cards proving ownership and value move from "helpful" to "decisive", because settlement runs on depreciated market value rather than replacement price.

Baggage delay is a different animal: the policy pays a benefit after the prescribed delay hours and, in the "smart delayed baggage" editions, covers interim essentials — toiletries, a change of clothes — bought while the bag is in transit. Keep those purchase bills; they are the claim. Once the bag is delivered, the delay benefit stops accruing. If the bag never arrives and is declared lost, the file converts to the loss indemnity with the PIR, the airline's written non-traceability confirmation, and the contents list with invoices. Where the loss is theft-linked at destination, a local police report joins the file — most wordings require it for personal-effects claims specifically.

Medical claims abroad: cashless where you can, complete file where you cannot

Emergency hospitalisation overseas runs through ICICI Lombard's assistance line first — the policy's global assistance partner authorises treatment at network facilities where the destination has them, and arranges direct billing where it does not. When cashless is impossible (common in remote itineraries), reimburse on return with the full chain: admission and discharge papers, itemised bills, pharmacy receipts, prescriptions, diagnostic reports and the attending physician's summary, plus a completed claim form and your EFT bank details. Two discipline points: adventure-activity and alcohol/drug exclusions are enforced through the medical records themselves, so undisclosed pre-existing conditions and activity histories resurface at this stage — the honest disclosure you made at purchase is what protects the file here. The mechanics: EFT details, stamped originals, retention

Three intake requirements trip first-time filers, and all three are printed on the claim forms themselves:

  • Bank account particulars are mandatory for EFT. IRDAI's circular on electronic transfer (IRDA/F&A/CIR/GLD/056/02/2014) makes the proposer's or policyholder's bank details compulsory for claim payout — one cancelled cheque or the IT-verified account proof, uploaded with the first filing, removes a full round-trip from the process.
  • Digital submissions need stamped scans. For digital claims, the forms direct you to scan and upload the original documents embossed "For ICICILombard Use Only" at the top-left — and to attach and submit every relevant document requested at submission rather than in instalments. Partial uploads read as incomplete files and queue behind complete ones.
  • Retain the originals. Even after digital settlement, the wordings reserve the right to call for originals on a need basis — keep the PIR printout, bills and carrier correspondence in a single folder for at least the period the policy specifies for claims.

Where travel claims go wrong

Rejections and queries we see most

  • Carrier correspondence missing — the delay is asserted, not evidenced
  • Threshold hours not met, or cause inside the annexure carve-outs (pre-notified technical, clearance, crew scheduling, civil unrest)
  • No PIR filed at the airport; baggage claim built from memory instead of the airline record
  • Contents claimed at replacement cost without invoices, then cut to depreciated value in assessment
  • Intimation after policy expiry, or late notification of a hospital admission
  • Non-refundable bookings claimed without proof the provider refused refund

What strengthens the file

  • PIR / delay certificate obtained before leaving the terminal
  • Boarding passes, ticket stubs and the full itinerary in one PDF
  • Itemised invoices with dates, plus card statements showing the charge
  • Police report at destination for theft-linked personal-effects loss
  • EFT bank particulars uploaded on day one; originals retained in one folder
  • Written intimation through a channel that gives you a reference number

If the claim stalls: the escalation ladder

Travel claims are governed by the same IRDAI settlement framework as every other non-life claim — complete files carry regulatory expectations on settlement timelines, and incomplete files legitimately do not. First move is always the same: request the clause-level query in writing and answer it in writing, attaching whatever document triggered the hold. If the reply goes unanswered or the rejection looks wrong on the face of the wordings, escalate through ICICI Lombard's grievance cell with the claim reference, then to IRDAI's designated grievance escalation, then the Insurance Ombudsman — a travel claim of modest size is squarely within ombudsman limits. Buying better: what actually changes the claim outcome

The claim is decided at purchase. Three choices carry more weight than any later paperwork: sum insured for medical — a US or Singapore admission can consume ₹20–50 lakh fast, so a ₹3–5 lakh medical sub-limit style configuration is a lottery ticket; buy medical cover sized to the destination's real costs. Single-trip versus multi-trip — a frequent flyer on an annual-multi-trip policy must still intimate each incident per trip; the structure changes, the proof requirements do not. Banking and contact details — an Indian mobile number and current EFT particulars that work from abroad keep the claim loop short. None of these appear in the comparison table on an aggregator page, and all three decide how the file runs when it counts.

What documents does an ICICI Lombard flight delay claim need?

The carrier's own confirmation or correspondence stating the delay and its circumstances is the primary document, supported by tickets, boarding passes and the policy copy. Claims filed only with app screenshots or memory of the announcement invite queries. Obtain the airline delay certificate at the airport before departing — retroactive requests are slower than the claim itself.

Why was my baggage claim paid below the value of the items lost?

Loss-of-checked-baggage covers are indemnity: settlement runs on depreciated market value against purchase invoices or warranty proof, not replacement cost, and the electronic-equipment extension has its own limits. A filed PIR, the contents list with dated invoices, and (for theft-linked loss at destination) a police report are what hold the assessed value up.

Do I need to inform the insurer before my trip ends?

Intimate as soon as the event is known — and where the wordings say so, before the policy expires. Late intimation gives the insurer a legitimate query and can compress the settlement window. Use a channel that produces a reference number: portal filing or the claims helpline with policy details at hand.

Are delays caused by technical problems or crew scheduling covered?

It depends on your edition's annexure. Common-carrier and flight-delay annexures typically carve out specified causes — equipment failure diagnosed before departure, clearance holds, crew or operational scheduling, civil unrest at either end — while paying qualifying delays past the prescribed hours. Read the annexure bound with your policy wordings; the qualifying-versus-excluded list is printed there and differs by edition.

What if ICICI Lombard rejects or stalls my travel claim?

Answer the query in writing with the clause referenced, then escalate in order: the insurer's grievance cell with your claim reference, IRDAI's grievance escalation, and finally the Insurance Ombudsman — travel claim amounts sit within ombudsman limits.
