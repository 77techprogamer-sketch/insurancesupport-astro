The one feature competitors undersell: post-maturity life cover

At maturity you receive sum assured + accumulated bonuses. But the base sum assured continues as free lifetime death cover — your nominee receives it again when you die, at no further premium. No other Indian insurance product does this. Competitor reviews price this as a modest IRR adjustment; for a family it is a permanent death benefit that survives after every rupee of "investment return" argument is settled.

Maturity claim: the practical checklist

  1. Original policy bond — mandatory. A duplicate takes 30+ days and an indemnity bond.
  2. Discharge voucher signed by every policyholder (and guardian, if the policy was assigned for a minor).
  3. NEFT mandate with cancelled cheque; LIC credits the bank account directly.
  4. PAN and Aadhaar — TDS applies where maturity proceeds exceed the section-194DA threshold.

LIC generally credits maturity proceeds within 7–15 working days of a complete document set. If your policy was bought before 2014 and premiums were paid partly in cash, expect an additional source-of-funds query. Where Jeevan Anand claims actually get stuck

Death claims on Plan 715 are settled under LIC's standard death claim framework — but three situations generate most disputes:

  • Claims within the first 3 policy years — full investigation into proposal disclosures. Misstatement of smoking, income, or prior insurance is the most common ground for repudiation.
  • Assignment or loan outstanding — a policy with an outstanding loan has a reduced net claim amount; nominees often mistake this for a deduction they can appeal.
  • Missing original bond in death claims — the duplicate-indemnity route works but delays payment by weeks.
Rejected death claim? LIC's internal review plus the four-stage IRDAI appeal path still recovers a meaningful share of repudiated claims — especially where the repudiation cites non-disclosure that is factually wrong. See the nominee's guide to rejected LIC death claims.

Buyer vs holder verdict

New buyers: if your goal is pure protection, term cover plus separate investment is mathematically stronger — the ~5% IRR is real. Existing holders with 15+ years paid: hold to maturity. Frequently asked questions

What happens to LIC Jeevan Anand after maturity?

The maturity amount (sum assured plus accumulated bonuses) is paid to you, and the base sum assured continues as free lifetime life cover for your nominee — no further premiums required.

How long does an LIC Jeevan Anand maturity claim take?

With a complete document set — original policy bond, signed discharge voucher, NEFT details, PAN and Aadhaar — LIC typically credits proceeds within 7 to 15 working days.

Can an LIC Jeevan Anand death claim be rejected in the first 3 years?

Yes. Claims in the first three policy years are investigated for misstatement in the proposal. A repudiation on this ground can be appealed through LIC's review and the IRDAI-mandated escalation path.

Is Jeevan Anand Plan 715 still available in 2026?

Yes, Plan 715 is the current version in force. Earlier versions (915, 815, 149) are closed to new proposals but continue to pay claims and maturities under their original terms.
