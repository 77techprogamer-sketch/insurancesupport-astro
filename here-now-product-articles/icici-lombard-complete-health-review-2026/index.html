ICICI Lombard Complete Health Insurance Review 2026: Tiers, Room Rent Limits and the Claim-Ratio Gap

Published 15 October 2026 · Independent product analysis · Reading time ~7 minutes

Review sites score ICICI Lombard Complete Health around 3.8–3.9 out of 5: solid product, competitive premiums on the standard tier, weaker claim settlement than the standalone health insurers. That score deserves unpacking, because the two numbers that drive it — 86.86% claim settlement ratio by number versus roughly 68% by value (FY 2024) — tell you exactly where this policy's claim risk lives.

The settlement-ratio gap, explained

86.86% settled by number but ~68% by value means small claims pay and large claims — the ones that actually threaten a family's finances — face far more friction. Typical friction points on Complete Health high-value claims:

  • Room-rent sub-limit on the standard tier — hospitalise in a single-private room under a capped plan and the bill is prorated; the deduction scales with the whole bill, not just the room difference.
  • Pre-existing conditions declared at proposal but questioned at claim — the 3-year wait gets enforced strictly; claims in year 2–3 for related diagnoses are common denial territory.
  • Day-2 claim intimation — cashless pre-authorisation paperwork errors (wrong diagnosis code, missing discharge summary) stall approvals at the TPA desk.

Cashless and reimbursement, in practice

ICICI Lombard runs one of India's largest cashless hospital networks with 24×7 claim support and app-based tracking. Cashless pre-authorisation follows the IRDAI 2016 cashless rules: decision within the stipulated window, and if the insurer seeks more information, they must specify what and why. When cashless is denied at admission, the reimbursement route requires original discharge summary, bills, prescriptions, and investigation reports — keep all of them; partial submission is the most common cause of a "pending" reimbursement that never closes.

Claim reduced or denied on a Complete Health policy? Proportionate deductions, PED waiting-period denials, and pre-auth failures are appealable — first to the insurer's grievance cell, then IRDAI's Bima Bharosa portal. Who Complete Health suits

Households already holding ICICI Lombard motor or travel policies wanting one-insurer simplicity, and buyers who choose Gold/Platinum where the clause quality matches the premium. If you are healthy, price-shopping the standard tier, and would file claims unaided — compare against the standalone health insurers first, where by-value settlement is materially stronger.

Frequently asked questions

What is ICICI Lombard Complete Health's claim settlement ratio?

For FY 2024, ICICI Lombard's health claim settlement ratio was approximately 86.86% by claim count and around 68% by claim value — a gap meaning larger claims face more scrutiny than smaller ones.

Does Complete Health Insurance have a room rent limit?

The Standard tier has room-rent sub-limits on lower sum-insured bands, creating proportionate-deduction risk. Gold and Platinum tiers have no room-rent cap at qualifying sum-insured levels.

What is the pre-existing disease waiting period in Complete Health?

Three years across the Standard, Gold, Platinum and iHealth variants. Arogya Sanjeevani, the IRDAI standard product offered by ICICI Lombard, has a 48-month PED wait.

How do I appeal a rejected ICICI Lombard health claim?

First file a written grievance with the insurer's grievance officer; unresolved after the statutory period, escalate to IRDAI's Bima Bharosa portal, then the insurance ombudsman. Keep the denial letter, policy document, and hospital records together for each stage.
