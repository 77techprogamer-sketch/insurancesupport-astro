How Supreme claims actually play out

Cashless

Pre-authorisation follows IRDAI's 2016 cashless framework: the insurer must approve, query, or reject within the prescribed windows. In practice, Supreme claims stall at the query stage — Care asks for additional documents (detailed diagnosis, prior reports, fitness certificate for seniors) and the clock restarts while the patient's family hunts for paperwork. Ask the hospital's insurance desk to submit the complete discharge summary at pre-auth itself; partial submissions are the main avoidable delay.

Reimbursement

Original bills, discharge summary, prescriptions, investigation reports, and pharmacy bills — all originals. Reimbursement claims submitted piecemeal sit "under process" indefinitely. Submit once, complete.

Where Supreme claims get cut

  • PED-related admissions inside the waiting window — the standard 3-year PED wait applies; claims for related diagnoses in years 1–2 are routinely denied.
  • Sub-limit products sold alongside Supreme — Care's senior and value variants carry cataract and knee-replacement sub-limits; buyers confusing the variants find hard caps they didn't expect.
  • Non-disclosure at proposal — diabetes, hypertension, and prior hospitalisation not declared at proposal is the most appealable denial ground.

Verdict

Care Supreme's feature set — no room-rent cap, unlimited restoration, broad day-care — is genuinely strong for its price band, and for straightforward hospitalisation claims it performs. The complaint ratio tells you what to do: buy Supreme with complete, honest disclosures, keep every original document, and pre-empt the pre-auth query stage. If a claim goes sideways, escalate early and in writing — Care responds to paper trails far faster than to phone follow-ups.

Frequently asked questions

Does Care Supreme have a room rent limit?

No. Care Supreme covers single private AC room category with no room-rent sub-limit, removing the proportionate-deduction risk that affects capped plans.

What is Care Health's claim settlement ratio?

Care Health's individual claim settlement ratio was around 93% by claim count for FY 2023-24, with an incurred claim ratio near 59% and 43 complaints per 10,000 claims — above the industry median.

How long does a Care Supreme cashless pre-authorisation take?

Under IRDAI's cashless framework the insurer must approve, query or reject within prescribed windows. Most delays occur at the additional-information query stage; submitting complete documents at pre-auth avoids this.

What is the pre-existing disease waiting period in Care Supreme?

The standard three-year PED waiting period applies. Claims for conditions related to an undisclosed pre-existing disease can also attract non-disclosure scrutiny.
