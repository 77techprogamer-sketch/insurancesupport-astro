ICICI Lombard Car Insurance Claim 2026: Cashless vs Reimbursement, Documents, Payout Maths

Cashless or reimbursement, the payout is decided by a file — deductible, depreciation and documentation determine what the garage actually gets paid.

A car insurance claim is a race against documentation: the damage is decided in an evening, but the file takes a week to build, and the file — not the damage — determines what is paid. ICICI Lombard runs India's largest private motor book, and its claim machinery splits cleanly in two: cashless repair at a network garage (the garage is paid directly, you settle the deductible) and reimbursement for repairs anywhere (you pay first, get reimbursed later). This guide walks both paths end to end — intimation in the app, the surveyor's 24–48 hour window, the document set due within seven days, the depreciation and deductible maths that shapes the final figure, and the appeal route when a motor claim comes back rejected.

First hour: intimation, not investigation

The claim starts the moment you intimate, and the channels are built for exactly this: the ICICI Lombard website or the IL Take Care mobile app, with the policy number, vehicle registration number, incident date and time, a short description, and photographs of the damage uploaded directly. The system generates a claim reference number immediately — screenshot it, because every later conversation quotes that number. Where the incident involves another party, a third-party injury, or theft, the police FIR joins the file at this stage rather than after the surveyor.

Cashless is the faster lane — reported averages run near 4–5 days for clean claims — but it exists only at network garages, and consumables (oil, filters, brake fluid) fall outside unless your cover includes them.

Reimbursement buys you garage choice and costs you time, cash-flow, and sometimes a reasonable-cost haircut — the assessor may trim rates above the local benchmark for your vehicle.

The order matters: intimate, then repair. A vehicle repaired before intimation or survey has no inspectable evidence, and the insurer is within its rights to question the causal link between the reported incident and the claimed damage. For accident scenes away from home, most policies also include towing provisions — ask the assistance desk before moving the vehicle under your own arrangements.

The document set: due within the week

ICICI Lombard's claim documentation expects, within roughly seven days of the incident:

  • Duly filled and signed claim form (available online or at the garage)
  • Copy of the vehicle's Registration Certificate (RC)
  • Copy of the driving licence of the person driving at the time
  • Copy of the valid ICICI Lombard policy certificate
  • FIR copy, where applicable — mandatory for theft, vandalism and third-party incidents
  • Photographs of the damage (the app upload plus hard copies in the file)
  • Garage repair estimate — for cashless this travels garage-to-insurer; for reimbursement it is part of your submission
  • Original repair invoice and payment receipt — the reimbursement backbone

Two documents quietly decide more motor claims than any other: the driving licence (an expired or absent licence converts an accident into a repudiation, no matter how genuine the damage) and the policy certificate (a lapsed or mis-classified policy — private versus commercial use — is the other classic kill). Both are checkable before you leave home; check them.

What actually gets paid: deductible, depreciation, IDV

The settlement figure is not the repair bill. Three adjustments sit between them:

₹ — illustrative split

Deductible per your policy schedule (commonly ₹1,000–₹2,000 for cars, higher for larger engines); depreciation follows IRDAI norms by part age and type. Add-on covers — consumables, zero-depreciation — close these gaps at premium time.

The deductible is contractual: a compulsory one per your policy schedule, plus any voluntary deductible you chose at purchase to lower the premium — every rupee of voluntary deductible is a rupee you fund at claim. Depreciation is regulatory, not discretionary: rubber, plastic, glass and fibre parts age on a schedule, and a ten-year-old bumper settles at a fraction of catalogue price. This is where add-on covers earn their renewal premium — zero-depreciation for near-new vehicles and high-part-cost models, consumables for the oil-and-filters layer, and engine-protect for monsoon water-ingestion claims, which standard policies exclude. Total-loss and theft claims follow a different ruler entirely: the IDV — insured declared value — governs, which is why the IDV at renewal is the single most consequential number in a motor policy.

Depreciation follows IRDAI's normative schedule rather than the garage's invoice: rubber, plastic and fibre components are typically depreciated at 50%, metal parts at roughly 5% per year of vehicle age, and glass on its own schedule — which is why a five-year-old bumper settles near half its catalogue figure while a structural panel loses much less. The exact rates are published in your policy's motor chapter and every assessor applies them mechanically; the practical lever is not disputing depreciation but buying it away: zero-depreciation cover for vehicles up to roughly five years old, consumables cover for the oil-and-filters layer, and engine protect for monsoon water-ingestion, which standard own-damage sections exclude outright.

Before the first claim of any kind, a five-minute kit check saves the five-day fight: policy certificate current, RC and driving licence photographed on your phone, the IL Take Care app installed and the policy number saved in it, and — if you drive after dark on unfamiliar roads — a dashcam or a habit of parking-lot photographs. The families who win motor claims are not the ones with better lawyers; they are the ones who never needed one, because the file assembled itself from what they already had.

Rejections, and the appeal that follows

Motor claim repudiations cluster around a handful of clauses: driving under the influence, racing or competitive use, using a private vehicle for commercial purposes without a commercial policy, driving without a valid licence, late or absent intimation, damage that predates the incident, and policy lapse. When a claim is rejected, ICICI Lombard issues a formal letter citing the specific clause — read the clause against your facts before replying, because the reply becomes the record.

Where motor claims fail

  • Repairs before intimation — evidence destroyed, causality unverifiable
  • Expired licence or lapsed policy discovered mid-file
  • Private vehicle used for commercial duty (ride-hailing, delivery) without the right policy
  • Pre-existing damage billed as new; dashcam or pre-incident photos would have settled it
  • Non-network reasonable-cost reductions accepted without objection

What strengthens the file

  • App intimation immediately, with the reference number saved
  • Scene photographs and dashcam clips before the vehicle moves
  • Valid licence, policy certificate and RC checked before the trip, not after
  • Written replies quoting the claim reference; written objection to cost cuts
  • Original invoice and payment trail for reimbursement claims

The ladder after rejection is standard and time-bound: an internal appeal to the Grievance Redressal Officer (keep the claim reference in every line), then IRDAI's grievance escalation, then the Insurance Ombudsman for amounts within its limits — motor claims of ordinary size sit squarely inside. How long does an ICICI Lombard car insurance claim take?

Cashless claims at network garages typically run about 4–5 days for clean files once the surveyor's estimate is approved; reimbursement claims average 10–15 working days from submission of the complete document set, since the insurer assesses reasonableness after you have already paid. Complex cases — third-party involvement, theft, disputed damage — add police and investigation time on top.

What documents are needed for an ICICI Lombard motor claim?

The signed claim form, RC copy, driving licence copy of the driver at the time, policy certificate, FIR copy where applicable, damage photographs, the garage estimate, and — for reimbursement — the original repair invoice with payment proof. Intimation through the IL Take Care app or website generates the claim reference number instantly; quote it in every follow-up.

Why is my settlement less than the repair bill?

Three deductions typically apply: the compulsory deductible from your policy schedule (plus any voluntary deductible you chose), regulatory depreciation on parts by age and type, and any reasonable-cost adjustment where garage rates exceed the benchmark for your make and model. Zero-depreciation and consumables add-ons close the first two gaps at premium time; written objection closes the third.

Can I repair my car before intimating the claim?

Do not. Intimate first — repairs begun before intimation or survey destroy the inspectable evidence linking the damage to the reported incident, and the insurer can legitimately question causality. For accidents away from home, call the assistance desk before towing or repairing; theft and vandalism additionally require the FIR before anything else proceeds.

My car claim was rejected. What are my options?

Read the rejection letter's cited clause against your facts, then reply in writing with the claim reference and evidence. If the answer does not move, escalate in order: the insurer's Grievance Redressal Officer, IRDAI's grievance portal, then the Insurance Ombudsman — ordinary motor claim amounts sit within ombudsman limits and the process is free. Keep every communication dated; escalation turns on the record, not the argument.

What is IDV in a car insurance policy?

Insured Declared Value — the current market value of your vehicle as fixed at each renewal, minus depreciation for age. It is the ceiling for total-loss and theft settlements (what the insurer pays when repair is uneconomic or the car is stolen), and the anchor for your premium. Setting IDV too low to save premium directly shrinks the total-loss payout; set it at the insurer's listed value for your model year.
