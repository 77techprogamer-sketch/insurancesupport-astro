The claim numbers that matter

  • Individual death CSR FY 2024-25: 98.15% — consistent above 98% for five years.
  • ₹24,420 crore paid in individual death claims in FY 2024-25 alone. At that scale, 98.15% means roughly 1 in 56 individual death claims did not settle in the year — and the families behind those are exactly who reads pages like this.
  • ~97% of death claims are paid within 30 days when the documentation is complete and the claim is straightforward.

How a LIC term death claim actually runs

  1. Intimation — nominee informs LIC (branch, licindia.in, or customer care) with the death certificate. Do this within days, not weeks.
  2. Document set — original policy bond or duplicate, death certificate, nominee KYC and bank details, claim form. For accidental death: FIR, panchnama/post-mortem report, and the driving licence where relevant.
  3. Investigation — claims within the first 3 policy years, or where the cause of death is disputed (accident vs illness, suicide clauses, drowning), trigger a fuller medical-and-background investigation.
  4. Settlement — straight claims credit within 30 days; investigated claims take longer, and LIC must communicate the status rather than sit silent.

Why term claims get rejected — and what the law says

The dominant ground remains non-disclosure in the proposal: smoking, pre-existing disease, income mismatch, or prior insurance not declared. Section 45 of the Insurance Act gives LIC a three-year contestability window from date of risk commencement — after three years, a policy cannot be challenged for misstatement except fraud. If a repudiation cites non-disclosure on a policy older than three years, it is contestable on that ground alone.

Nominee fighting a denial? The appeal ladder — grievance officer, IRDAI Bima Bharosa portal, then the insurance ombudsman — is time-bound and document-heavy. Choosing between 954 and 955

Cover identical; the difference is servicing. Online New Tech Term means you file claims yourself through the digital channel; New Jeevan Amar comes with an agent who handles paperwork — worth paying a slight premium differential for families unfamiliar with claim procedures. Whichever you choose, register the nominee carefully at proposal, and keep the bond with a second copy stored separately. Most delayed term claims are not disputes — they are missing-paper problems.

Frequently asked questions

What is LIC's claim settlement ratio for term insurance?

LIC's individual death claim settlement ratio was 98.15% for FY 2024-25, consistently above 98% for five consecutive years, per IRDAI-reported figures.

Can an LIC term insurance claim be rejected for non-disclosure?

Yes, within the first three years from risk commencement (Section 45, Insurance Act 1938). After three years the policy is contestable only on grounds of fraud.

How long does an LIC term death claim take to settle?

Straightforward claims with complete documentation are typically settled within 30 days. Claims involving investigation — first three policy years, or disputed cause of death — take longer, and LIC must keep the nominee informed of the status.

Which LIC term plan should I buy — New Tech Term or New Jeevan Amar?

Both offer identical benefit options. New Tech Term (954) is online-only and cheaper with self-service claims; New Jeevan Amar (955) is bought through an agent who assists with servicing and claim paperwork.
