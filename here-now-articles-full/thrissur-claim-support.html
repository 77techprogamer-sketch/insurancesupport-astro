Frequently Asked Questions

My claim was rejected. What should I do?
Ask the insurer for its written decision and the policy provision it relied on. Compare the explanation with your policy and claim records, then use the insurer’s grievance process if needed. External complaint routes have their own eligibility and deadlines. Support can help organize the file but cannot guarantee a reversal.
What documents should I gather for a claim?
Start with the policy schedule, claim form, claim reference, insurer’s written decision or query, and relevant correspondence. Add supporting medical records and bills for health claims, repair estimates and incident records for motor claims, or a death certificate and claimant documents for life claims. Confirm the final checklist with your insurer.
How long does claim settlement take?
There is no single timeline for every claim. Timing depends on the policy, claim type, completeness of documents, and whether the insurer needs more information or investigation. Keep dated proof of submissions and check your insurer’s written updates and current official rules.
